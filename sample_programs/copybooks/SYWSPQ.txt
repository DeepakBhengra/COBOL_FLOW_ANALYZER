      *            AND A RESPONSE ARRAY FOR THE QUANITY BREAK PRICES  *         
      *            FOR A SKU FOR USE BY APPLICATIONS THAT DISPLAY     *         
      *            OR REQUIRE QUANTITY BREAK DATA.                    *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  USAGE:    COPY IN YOUR PRICING APPLICATION AFTER THE         *         
      *            STRATEGIC PRICING HEADER AND USE IN CONJUNCTION    *         
      *            WITH UTILITY COPYBOOK SYSPMQ.                      *

      *****************************************************************         
      *    WORKING STORAGE - STRATEGIC PRICING QUANTITY BREAK                   
      *                      REQUEST AND RESPONSE AREAS                         
      *****************************************************************         
                                                                                
         02 SYWSPS-REQUEST-RESPONSE-AREAS.                                      
                                                                                
      ***************************************************************           
      *    THE STRATEGIC PRICING REQUEST AREA FOR A SINGLE SKU                  
      ***************************************************************           
                                                                                
           03 SYWSPS-REQUEST-GROUP.                                             
                                                                                
            04 SYWSPS-REQUEST-AREAS                                             
                 OCCURS 1 TIMES.                                                
               05 SYWSPS-REQUEST-AREA.                                          
                                                                                
      *    (THE REQUEST AREA CONTAINS THE SKU TO BE PRICED)                     
       COPY SYWSPSRQ.                                                           
                                                                                
      ***************************************************************           
      *    THE STRATEGIC PRICING RESPONSE AREAS FOR THE PRICE                   
      *    OF A SKU QUANTITY 1 AND UP TO 4 QUANTITY BREAK LEVELS                
      ***************************************************************           
                                                                                
           03 SYWSPS-RESPONSE-GROUP.                                            
                                                                                
            04 SYWSPS-RESPONSE-AREAS                                            
                 OCCURS 5 TIMES.                                                
               05 SYWSPS-RESPONSE-AREA.                                         
                                                                                
      *    (THE RESPONSE AREAS REPEAT TO RETURN EACH QTY BREAK ELEMENT)         
       COPY SYWSPSRS.
