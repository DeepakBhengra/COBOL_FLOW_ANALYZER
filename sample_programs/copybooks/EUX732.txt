000700*  PURPOSE:  COMPARE AN ADDRESS TO ITS SELF TO DETERMINE IF IT  *

001000*                                                               *

001200*                                                               *

001400*            ADDRESS                                            *         
001500*                                                               *         
001600* WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.                                      *         
001700* 01  WS-EUP732                  PIC  X(08) VALUE 'EUP732'.     *         
001800*                                                               *         
001900* COPY EUX732 REPLACING ==:EUX732:== BY ==EUX732==.             *         
002000*                                                               *         
002100* PROCEDURE DIVISION.                                           *         
002200*     INITIALIZE EUX732-PARMS                                   *         
002300*     MOVE EU-ADDR-1-TXT TO EUX732-ADDR-1-TXT-IN                *         
002400*     MOVE EU-ADDR-2-TXT TO EUX732-ADDR-2-TXT-IN                *         
002500*     MOVE EU-ADDR-3-TXT TO EUX732-ADDR-3-TXT-IN                *         
002600*                                                               *         
002700*     CALL WS-EUP732 USING EUX732-PARMS                         *         
002800*     IF EUX732-SCORE = 100                                     *         
002900*        THIS IS A VALID ADDRESS AND MAY BE INSERTED            *         
003000*     ELSE                                                      *         
003100*        THIS IS AN INVALID ADDRESS AND MAY NOT BE INSERTED     *         
003200*        INTO THE DATA BASE, SEND A MESSAGE TO THE USER THAT    *         
003300*        THEY SHOULD CONTACT IT.                                *
