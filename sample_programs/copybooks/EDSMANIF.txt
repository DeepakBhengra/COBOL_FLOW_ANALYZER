       EDSMANIF-100-TBXCARR-FOR-SCAC.                                           
                                                                                
              MOVE SPACES               TO WS-SCAC-CODE                         
              MOVE TC-COMPANY           TO TB-CARR-CO                           
              MOVE BTN-CARRIER-CODE     TO TB-CARR-CODE                         
              CALL 'SYU510'          USING TB-CARRIER-TABLE-IA                  
              IF TB-CARR-NORESP AND                                             
                 TB-CARR-SCAC-CODE > SPACE                                      
                 MOVE 'Y'               TO WS-TBXCARR-FOUND-SW                  
                 MOVE TB-CARR-SCAC-CODE TO WS-SCAC-CODE                         
              ELSE                                                              
                 MOVE 'N'               TO WS-TBXCARR-FOUND-SW                  
              END-IF.                                                           
                                                                                
       EDSMANIF-100-EXIT.                                                       
            EXIT.                                                               
                                                                                
       EDSMANIF-200-WRITE-TO-MQ.                                                
                                                                                
    *** THIS PROGRAM DOES NOT USE A CORRELATION QUEUE TO TRIGGER THE            
    *** BASE QUEUE PROCESSING. SPACES TO WS-MQ-CORRELATION-NAME ENSURES         
    *** THAT THE CORRELATION ID LOGIC DOESN'T GET HIT.                          
                                                                                
            MOVE SPACES                 TO WS-MQ-CORRELATION-NAME               
            PERFORM 8000-MQ-INITIALIZATION                                      
                                                                                
            MOVE WS-QN-LITERAL          TO WS-MQ-BASE-NAME                      
            PERFORM 8000-MQ-INIT-BASE-QUEUE                                     
                                                                                
            COMPUTE MQUTIL-DATA-LENGTH = LENGTH OF BTN-TRKMST                   
            MOVE BTN-TRKMST              TO MQPDBR-DATA                         
            PERFORM 8100-MQ-PUT-BASE-MESSAGE                                    
            PERFORM 8200-MQ-TERMINATE-BASE-QUEUE.                               
                                                                                
       EDSMANIF-200-EXIT.                                                       
            EXIT.
