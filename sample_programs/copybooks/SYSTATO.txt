           MOVE 'N'                    TO SYWTAT-FOUND-SW                       
                                          SYWTAT-FAILURE.                       
           PERFORM VARYING SYWTAT-IX FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL                          
             SYWTAT-FOUND OR SYWTAT-IX > 10                                     
               IF SYWTAT-IX = 10                                                
                   MOVE 'TC'           TO SYWTAT-CARRIER-CODE                   
               ELSE                                                             
                   MOVE SYWTAT-IX      TO SYWTAT-CODE                           
               END-IF                                                           
                                                                                
               MOVE SYWTAT-COUNTRY     TO TB-PCODE-COMPANY-CODE                 
               MOVE SYWTAT-ZIP-PREF    TO TB-PCODE-POSTAL-CODE                  
               MOVE SYWTAT-CARRIER-CODE                                         
                                       TO TB-PCODE-CARRIER-CODE                 
               CALL 'SYU510'           USING TB-PCODE-PROC-IA                   
               IF TB-PCODE-NORESP                                               
                   MOVE 'Y'            TO SYWTAT-FOUND-SW                       
               END-IF                                                           
           END-PERFORM.                                                         
                                                                                
           IF SYWTAT-NOT-FOUND                                                  
               MOVE 'OT'               TO SYWTAT-CARRIER-CODE                   
               MOVE 'Y'                TO SYWTAT-FAILURE                        
           END-IF.
