       A601-FORMAT-PGM-TYPE.                                                    
           MOVE SPACES                   TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE                 
           EVALUATE TRUE                                                        
             WHEN  ICWCOP-VEND-MAIN-PRG                                         
             WHEN  ICWCOP-PART-MAIN-PRG                                         
              MOVE ICWCOP-IM-MAIN-PGM-CD TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE1                
             WHEN  ICWCOP-VEND-SUB-PRG                                          
             WHEN  ICWCOP-PART-SUB-PRG                                          
              MOVE ICWCOP-IM-MAIN-PGM-CD TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE1                
              MOVE ICWCOP-EU-MRKT-SGMT-TYP-CD                                   
                                         TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE2                
             WHEN ICWCOP-VEND-CUST-PRG                                          
             WHEN ICWCOP-PART-CUST-PRG                                          
              MOVE ICWCOP-IM-MAIN-PGM-CD TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE1                
              MOVE ICWCOP-VEND-ACOP-PGM-CD                                      
                                         TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE2                
           END-EVALUATE.
