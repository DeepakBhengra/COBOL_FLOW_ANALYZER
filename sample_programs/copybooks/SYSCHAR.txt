      *    SWEEP TEXT FIELD FOR INVALID EBCDIC CHARACTERS.                      
      *                                                                         
      *          ***  NEEDED FROM CALLING PROGRAM   ***                         
      *    MOVE (TEXT FIELD)          TO VC-TEXT-AREA.   (REQUIRED)             
      *    MOVE (TEXT FIELD LENGTH)   TO VC-SUB-MAX.     (OPTIONAL)             
      *                                            (MAX IS DEFAULT)             
      *                                                                         
      *          ***  AVAILABLE TO CALLING PROGRAM  ***                         
      *    ERROR RETURNED WHEN ONE FIELD IS FOUND TO HAVE A                     
      *    NON-PRINTABLE CHAR.                                                  
      * ***********************************************************             
       V100-CLEANUP-TEXT.                                                       
           IF  VC-SUB-MAX                   > +2048                             
               MOVE +2048                   TO VC-SUB-MAX.                      
           MOVE 'N'                         TO VC-TXT-CHANGED-SW.               
           PERFORM V200-SWEEP-CHARACTERS    THRU V200-EXIT                      
              VARYING VC-SUB FROM 1 BY 1                                        
              UNTIL VC-SUB               > VC-SUB-MAX                           
              OR    VC-INVLD-CHAR-COUNT  > 0.                                   
                                                                                
       V100-EXIT.  EXIT.                                                        
      *                                                                         
      * ***********************************************************             
      *     FOR EACH CHARACTER IN A TEXT FIELD DO A BINARY SEARCH               
      *     OF ALL VALID CHARACTERS REPLACING INVALIED CHARACTERS               
      *     WITH SPACE.                                                         
      * ***********************************************************             
       V200-SWEEP-CHARACTERS.                                                   
           SEARCH ALL VALID-CHARS                                               
               AT END                                                           
                  MOVE VC-SUB               TO VC-TXT-OFFSET-COUNT              
                  ADD  +1                   TO VC-INVLD-CHAR-COUNT              
               WHEN                                                             
                  VALID-CHAR (VCHAR-INDX)    = VC-TXT-CHAR (VC-SUB)             
                  ADD  +1                   TO VC-VALID-CHAR-COUNT              
           END-SEARCH.                                                          
       V200-EXIT.  EXIT.
