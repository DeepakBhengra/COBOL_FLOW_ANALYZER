      *                                                                *        
      *================================================================*        
      *                                                                *        
      * PURPOSE:                                                       *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  THIS COPYBOOK IS USED TO CONTROL THE CONVERSION OF REMOTE     *        
      *  COSTS TO LOCAL COSTS.  THIS COPYBOOK CAN ONLY WORK IF THE     *        
      *  PROGRAM USES INVENTORY SHARING LOGIC, THE STANDARD INVENTORY  *        
      *  SHARING COPYBOOKS (ECW*), AND THE REGIONALIZED INVENTORY      *        
      *  TOOLKIT COPYBOOK (CURRENTLY CALLED ICSCRI2).                  *        
      *                                                                *        
      * REVISION INFO:                                                 *        
      * 07/30/01  WFP  NEW COPYBOOK.                                   *WFP01211
      *                                                                *        
      *================================================================*        
      *                                                                *        
      ***                                                        ***            
      ***  THIS PARAGRAPH WILL DETERMINE IF REMOTE COSTS SHOULD  ***            
      ***  BE USED, AND IF THE CONVERSION PROGRAMS NEEDS TO BE   ***            
      ***  CALLED.                                               ***            
      ***                                                        ***            
                                                                                
       8000-ICRI-SET-IH-DATA-SUPER.                                             
                                                                                
      *===>  IF THE LOCAL COMPANY IS NOT SET FOR INVENTORY SHARING,             
      *===>  THEN WE CANNOT USE THE REGIONALIZED INVENTORY LOGIC.               
      *===>  SINCE THIS COPYBOOK SHOULD ONLY BE USED BY INVENTORY               
      *===>  SHARING PROGRAMS, THEN REAL-TIME COSTS MUST BE NEEDED.             
      *===>  IN SUCH A CASE, THE COSTS MUST BE OBTAINED FROM THE                
      *===>  REMOTE DATABASE, OR ELSE THE REGIONALIZED INVENTORY                
      *===>  LOGIC CANNOT WORK.                                                 
                                                                                
           IF TB-CPO-ALT-INVENTORY-ALLOWED                                      
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              MOVE 'XX'                                                         
                TO ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                          
              GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT.                                             
                                                                                
      *===>  IF THE LOCAL COMPANY CODE HAS NOT BEEN SET, THEN THIS              
      *===>  MUST BE THE FIRST RUN OF THE PROGRAM.  SO, INITIALIZE              
      *===>  THE FULL I-O AREA, AND SET THE COMPANY CODE, WHICH WILL            
      *===>  FORCE A THE CALL OF SYU889.                                        
                                                                                
           IF TC-COMPANY = WS-INAL2-CO                                          
              MOVE 'Y'                                                          
                TO ICRI-SKU-STATUS-SW                                           
           ELSE                                                                 
           IF ICRI-FORCE-PROCESSING                                             
              MOVE 'N'                                                          
                TO ICRI-SKU-STATUS-SW                                           
           ELSE                                                                 
              MOVE 'N'                                                          
                TO ICRI-SKU-STATUS-SW                                           
              GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT.                                             
                                                                                
           IF WS-INSHED-NORESP                                                  
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              MOVE 'N'                                                          
                TO ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                          
              GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT.                                             
                                                                                
           IF INI-COMPONENT-BUSINESS-SW > SPACES                                
            OR ICRI-FORCE-PROCESSING                                            
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              MOVE 'XX'                                                         
                TO ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                          
              GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT.                                             
                                                                                
           IF ICRI-LOC-CO > SPACES                                              
            AND ICRI-LOC-CO = TC-COMPANY                                        
              IF ICRI-LOC-PART > SPACES                                         
               AND WS-INAL2-PART = ICRI-LOC-PART                                
                 GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT                                           
              ELSE                                                              
                 INITIALIZE ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                 
                 INITIALIZE ICRI-LAST-BRANCH-INSBRN                             
           ELSE                                                                 
              INITIALIZE ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                    
              INITIALIZE ICRI-LAST-BRANCH-INSBRN                                
              MOVE 'Y'                                                          
                TO ICRI-SKU-STATUS-SW                                           
              MOVE TC-COMPANY                                                   
                TO ICRI-LOC-CO.                                                 
                                                                                
      *===>  ENSURE THAT IH COSTS HAVE BEEN SET.                                
                                                                                
      *===>  IF THE INVENTORY HOLDING COSTS HAVE NOT ALREADY BEEN               
      *===>  OBTAINED AND CONVERTED, THEN GET THE REMOTE INSHED                 
      *===>  SEGMENT AND CONVERT THE COSTS.  AFTERWARDS, REPOSTION              
      *===>  THE PCB POINTER TO THE LOCAL PCB, AND OBTAIN THE LOCAL             
      *===>  INSHED SEGMENT.                                                    
                                                                                
           IF ICRI-DO-NOT-USE-RI                                                
              GO TO 8000-ICRI-EXIT                                              
           ELSE                                                                 
           IF ICRI-LOC-PART > SPACES                                            
            AND WS-INAL2-PART = ICRI-LOC-PART                                   
            AND ICRI-DATA-FOUND                                                 
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              PERFORM 8200-ICRI-INIT-COST-CONV-DATA                             
                 THRU 8200-ICRI-EXIT                                            
              IF ICRI-DATA-FOUND                                                
                 MOVE ICRI-INV-OWNING-COMPANY                                   
                   TO WS-INAL2-CO                                               
                      WS-PCB-REGION                                             
                 MOVE 'I2'                                                      
                   TO WS-PCB-TYPE                                               
                 PERFORM 8888-SETUP-DLI-CALL                                    
                    THRU 8888-SETUP-DLI-CALL-EXIT                               
                 PERFORM 1550-GU-INSHED-AL2-ONLY                                
                 IF WS-INSHED-NORESP                                            
                    PERFORM 8400-ICRI-CONV-COST-DATA                            
                       THRU 8400-ICRI-EXIT                                      
                 ELSE                                                           
                    MOVE 'N'                                                    
                      TO ICRI-DATA-STATUS-SW                                    
                 END-IF                                                         
                 MOVE ICRI-LOC-CO                                               
                   TO WS-INAL2-CO                                               
                      WS-PCB-REGION                                             
                 PERFORM 8888-SETUP-DLI-CALL                                    
                    THRU 8888-SETUP-DLI-CALL-EXIT                               
                 PERFORM 1550-GU-INSHED-AL2-ONLY.                               
                                                                                
       8000-ICRI-EXIT.                                                          
                                                                                
           EXIT.
