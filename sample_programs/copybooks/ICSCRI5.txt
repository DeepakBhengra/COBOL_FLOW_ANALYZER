      ***  CALL THE REGIONALIZED INVENTORY COST CONV SUBROUTINE   ***           
      ***  FROM A CICS PROGRAM.                                   ***           
      ***                                                         ***           
                                                                                
       8300-ICRI-CALL-COST-CONV-PROG.                                           
                                                                                
           MOVE 'SYU889  '                                                      
             TO ICRI-REG-INV-COST-PROGRAM.                                      
                                                                                
           CALL ICRI-REG-INV-COST-PROGRAM USING DFHEIBLK,                       
                                                DFHCOMMAREA,                    
                                                ICRI-PROGRAM-INTERFACE.         
                                                                                
       8300-ICRI-EXIT.                                                          
                                                                                
           EXIT.
