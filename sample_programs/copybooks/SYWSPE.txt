      *         WORKING STORAGE FOR STRATEGIC PRICING - EXPLAIN ID    *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  PURPOSE:  COMMUNICATION AREA CONTAINING THE EXPLAIN ID       *         
      *            RETURNED FROM THE PRICING ENGINE WHEN              *         
      *            SYWSPH-SAVE-EXPLAIN-FLAG = 'Y'.                    *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  USAGE:    COPY IN YOUR PRICING APPLICATION AFTER THE         *         
      *            STRATEGIC PRICING HEADER AND SKU AREAS.            *         
      *            SET SYWSPH-SAVE-EXPLAIN-FLAG = 'Y' BEFORE          *         
      *            INVOKING THE ENGINE CONNECTOR/PRICING ENGINE.      *         
      *            THIS DATA AREA IS USED BY THE ORS697WM COPYBOOK    *         
      *            TO PASS DATA TO THE ORP697 EXPLAIN WRITER.         *         
      *                                                               *         
      *****************************************************************
