      ******************************************************************        
      ******************************************************************        
      * ** FIXED DATE CALCULATION:                                     *        
      *- 1. IF THE FIXED DATE IS BETTER THAN INVOICE DATE              *        
      *-    USE IT FOR THE DISC DATE AND DUE DATE                      *        
      * ****************************************************************        
       902-DETERMINE-FIXED-DATE.                                                
                                                                                
           CALL 'CAWINUP'                                                       
               USING TB-TERMS-FIXED-DATE-DATE                                   
                     DEFAULT-WINDOW-SPEC                                        
                     DECIMAL5-TYPE                                              
                     TB-TERMS-FIXED-DATE-DATE-Y2K                               
                     THIS-YEAR-Y2K.                                             
           CALL 'CAWINUP'                                                       
               USING WS-INPUT-DISC-DATE                                         
                     DEFAULT-WINDOW-SPEC                                        
                     DECIMAL5-TYPE                                              
                     WS-INPUT-DISC-DATE-Y2K                                     
                     THIS-YEAR-Y2K.                                             
                                                                                
           IF TB-TERMS-FIXED-DATE-DATE-Y2K  GREATER THAN                        
                                          WS-INPUT-DISC-DATE-Y2K                
               MOVE TB-TERMS-FIXED-DATE-DATE                                    
                                   TO WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE                       
                                      WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE                        
           ELSE                                                                 
               MOVE WS-INPUT-DISC-DATE                                          
                                   TO WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE                       
               MOVE WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE                                           
                                   TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE

      ***>>>>>>>         PROX TERM               <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<***        
      ******************************************************************        
      ******************************************************************        
      * ** PROX TERM DUE DATE CALCULATION:                             *        
      *- 1. USE TB-TERMS-PROX-DISC-DAY OR TB-TERMS-PROX-DUE-DAY        *        
      *-    TO DETERMINE THE DUE DAY OR DISC DAY                       *        
      *- 2. COMPARE THE INVOICE DAY TO THE TB-TERMS-PROX-CUTOFF        *        
      *-    TO DETERMINE THE DUE MONTH OR DISC MONTH                   *        
      *-    IF PASSED THE CUTOFF THEN ADD +2  MONTH                    *        
      *-       OTHERWISE         THEN ADD +1  MONTH                   *         
      * ****************************************************************        
       903-DETERMINE-PROX-DATE.                                                 
           MOVE  WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE     TO  SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE.              
           PERFORM A98-CONVERT-JULIAN-TO-GREG-US THRU                           
                   A98-EXIT.                                                    
                                                                                
           MOVE  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6    TO WS-TERMS-DISC-DATE                    
                                          WS-TERMS-DUE-DATE.                    
                                                                                
           MOVE TB-TERMS-PROX-DISC-DAY TO WS-DI-DA.                             
           IF  TB-TERMS-PROX-DUE-DAY GREATER THAN ZEROES                        
               MOVE TB-TERMS-PROX-DUE-DAY                                       
                                       TO WS-DU-DA                              
           ELSE                                                                 
               MOVE WS-DI-DA           TO WS-DU-DA.                             
                                                                                
           IF  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD    >  TB-TERMS-PROX-CUTOFF                 
               ADD 2                   TO WS-DU-MO WS-DI-MO                     
           ELSE                                                                 
               ADD 1                   TO WS-DU-MO WS-DI-MO.                    
           IF  WS-DU-MO GREATER THAN 12                                         
               SUBTRACT 12           FROM WS-DU-MO                              
               ADD 1                   TO WS-DU-YR.                             
           IF  WS-DI-MO GREATER THAN 12                                         
               SUBTRACT 12           FROM WS-DI-MO                              
               ADD 1                   TO WS-DI-YR.                             
                                                                                
           MOVE  WS-TERMS-DISC-DATE    TO SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.            
           PERFORM A99-CONVERT-GREG-US-TO-JULIAN THRU                           
                   A99-EXIT.                                                    
           MOVE  SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE TO  WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE.                
                                                                                
           MOVE  WS-TERMS-DUE-DATE     TO SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.            
           PERFORM A99-CONVERT-GREG-US-TO-JULIAN THRU                           
                   A99-EXIT.                                                    
           MOVE SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE TO  WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.

      ******************************************************************        
      ******************************************************************        
      * ** DAY TERMS DUE DATE CALCULATION:                             *        
      *- 1. DIRECT DEBIT CREDIT TERMS DAYS IS USED TO DETERMINE DUE DATE        
      *-    FOR CREDIT MEMO ONLY FOR DIRECT DEBIT CUSTOMERS            *

       904-DETERMINE-DAY-TERMS.                                                 
      *                                                                         
      *** DAY TERMS DISC DATE CALCULATION:                             *        
      *                                                                         
           COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE EQUAL                                    
                              WS-INPUT-DISC-DATE + TB-TERMS-DISC-DAYS.          
                                                                                
           MOVE WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE      TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE.                 
           DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                    
                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                     
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER.                                  
           IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                            
                  ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                            
                  ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           END-IF.                                                              
           MOVE WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE TO WS-OUTPUT-DISC-DATE.                      
      *                                                                         
      ***  DUE DATE CALCULATION                                                 
      *                                                                         
           IF  TB-TERMS-CODE > 699 AND                                          
               TB-TERMS-CODE < 800 AND                                          
               WS-INPUT-CREDIT-MEMO = 'Y' AND                                   
               WS-INPUT-CREDIT-REASON NOT = 'DR'                                
               COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE =                                     
                       WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE + TB-TERMS-DIRDEB-CREDT-DAYS           
           ELSE                                                                 
               IF  TB-TERMS-DUE-DAYS  >  ZEROES                                 
                   COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE =                                 
                           WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE + TB-TERMS-DUE-DAYS                
               ELSE                                                             
                   MOVE WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE    TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.     SCT04320
           MOVE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE       TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE.                 
           DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                    
                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                     
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER.                                  
           IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                            
                  ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                            
                  ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           END-IF.                                                              
           MOVE WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.

      ***          END  OF  MONTH  TERMS  EOM                        ***        
      ******************************************************************        
      * ** EOM TERMS DUE DATE CALCULATION:                             *        
      *--- 1. CALCULATE DUE MONTH BY TB-TERMS-DUE-DAYS / 30            *        
      *--- 2. SET THE 'LAST' DAY OF THE MONTH IN WHICH DUE FALLS       *        
      *--- 3. ADD TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS TO DUE DATE ONLY IF APPLICABLE*        
      ******************************************************************

      *----------------------------------------------------------------*        
      *-   EXAMPLE, INV-DATE ON DEC 31 AND TERM DAYS IS 30 DAYS       -*        
      *-   DUE DATE WILL BE ON JAN 31TH NEXT YEAR.                    -*        
      *----------------------------------------------------------------*        
                                                                                
           MOVE  WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE     TO  SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE.              
           PERFORM A98-CONVERT-JULIAN-TO-GREG-US THRU                           
                   A98-EXIT.                                                    
                                                                                
           COMPUTE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM =                                      
                   SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM + (TB-TERMS-DUE-DAYS / 30).            
                                                                                
           IF  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM > 12                                       
               SUBTRACT 12             FROM SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM               
               ADD      01             TO   SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY               
           END-IF.                                                              
                                                                                
           IF  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM    =  01 OR 03 OR 05 OR 07 OR              
                                           08 OR 10 OR 12                       
               MOVE  31                TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD                
           ELSE                                                                 
               IF SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM =  04 OR 06 OR 09 OR 11                 
                   MOVE 30             TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD                
               ELSE                                                             
                   DIVIDE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY BY 4                            
                       GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER                                   
                   IF  WS-REMAINDER > 0                                         
                       MOVE 28         TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD                
                   ELSE                                                         
                       MOVE 29         TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD.               
                                                                                
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD  TO  SYU1-DDMMYY-DATE-6-DD                
                                           SYU1-YYMMDD-DATE-6-DD.               
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM  TO  SYU1-DDMMYY-DATE-6-MM                
                                           SYU1-YYMMDD-DATE-6-MM.               
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY  TO  SYU1-DDMMYY-DATE-6-YY                
                                           SYU1-YYMMDD-DATE-6-YY.               
                                                                                
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6 TO SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.                
           PERFORM A99-CONVERT-GREG-US-TO-JULIAN THRU                           
                   A99-EXIT.                                                    
           MOVE SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE TO  WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.                  
                                                                                
           IF  TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS NUMERIC                                   
               COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE =  WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE    +            
                                             TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS             
                                                                                
               MOVE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE    TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
               DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                
                      GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                 
                           REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER                               
               IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                             
                  IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                        
                      ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE             
                  END-IF                                                        
               ELSE                                                             
                  IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                        
                      ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE             
                  END-IF                                                        
               END-IF                                                           
               MOVE WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.

                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK

              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365

           DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                    
                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                     
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER.                                  
           IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                            
                  ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                            
                  ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           END-IF.                                                              
           MOVE WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE                        
                                      SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE.                   
           PERFORM A98-CONVERT-JULIAN-TO-GREG-US THRU                           
                   A98-EXIT.                                                    
                                                                                
           IF SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD     =  TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS               
               NEXT SENTENCE                                                    
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD  <  TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS               
                  MOVE TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS                                   
                                       TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD

                                       TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD

                      MOVE 01             TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM             
                      ADD  01             TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY

                      ADD  01             TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM.            
                                                                                
           IF SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM  =  2                                       
            AND SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD  >  28

                     MOVE 28           TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD                
                 ELSE                                                           
                     MOVE 29           TO  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD                
                 END-IF                                                         
           END-IF.                                                              
                                                                                
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD  TO  SYU1-DDMMYY-DATE-6-DD

                                           SYU1-YYMMDD-DATE-6-MM.               
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY  TO  SYU1-DDMMYY-DATE-6-YY                
                                           SYU1-YYMMDD-DATE-6-YY.               
                                                                                
           MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6 TO SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.                
           PERFORM A99-CONVERT-GREG-US-TO-JULIAN THRU                           
                   A99-EXIT.

      ***          CUSTOMER DUE QUARTER TERMS               ***                 
      *********************************************************                 
      *- CUSTOMER DUE QUARTER TERMS CODES IMPLIES THE FOLLOWING:                
      *- 1. MAKE THE DUE MONTH TO NEXT MONTH OF END QUARTER                     
      *- 2. COMPUTE THE DUE DATE START WITH THE 1ST DAY OF THE                  
      *-    DUE MONTH BY ADDING TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS                          
      *- 3. IF TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS = 0                                       
      *-    MAKE THE INVOICE DAY AS THE DUE DAY                                 
      *********************************************************                 
       907-CALC-CUST-QUARTER-TERMS.                                             
                                                                                
           COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE   =  WS-INPUT-DUE-DATE    +               
                                           TB-TERMS-DUE-DAYS.                   
                                                                                
           MOVE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE     TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE.                   
           DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                    
                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                     
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER.                                  
           IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                            
                  ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                            
                  ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           END-IF.                                                              
           MOVE WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE TO WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE                        
                                      SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE.                   
           PERFORM A98-CONVERT-JULIAN-TO-GREG-US THRU                           
                   A98-EXIT.                                                    
           MOVE  SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6    TO WS-TERMS-DUE-DATE.                    
                                                                                
           EVALUATE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM                                       
               WHEN  1 THRU   3                                                 
                    MOVE  4            TO WS-DU-MO                              
               WHEN  4 THRU   6                                                 
                    MOVE  7            TO WS-DU-MO                              
               WHEN  7 THRU   9                                                 
                    MOVE  10           TO WS-DU-MO                              
               WHEN 10 THRU  12                                                 
                    MOVE  1            TO WS-DU-MO                              
                    ADD   1            TO WS-DU-YR                              
           END-EVALUATE.                                                        
                                                                                
      * RESET TO THE 1ST DAY OF THE DUE MONTH                                   
           MOVE 1                      TO WS-DU-DA.                             
                                                                                
      * CONVERT TO JULIAN                                                       
           MOVE WS-TERMS-DUE-DATE  TO SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.                
           PERFORM A99-CONVERT-GREG-US-TO-JULIAN THRU                           
                   A99-EXIT.                                                    
           MOVE SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE TO  WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE.                  
                                                                                
           IF TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS = 0                                        
      * DEFAULT SET TO THE 30TH DAY                                             
              COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE =                                      
                      WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE + 30  - 1                              
           ELSE                                                                 
      * USE EXTENDED DAY                                                        
              COMPUTE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE =                                      
                      WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE + TB-TERMS-EXTENDED-DAYS - 1           
           END-IF.                                                              
                                                                                
           MOVE WS-OUTPUT-DUE-DATE     TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE.                   
           DIVIDE WS-TERMS-COMP-YEAR    BY 4                                    
                  GIVING WS-LEAP-YEAR-CHECK                                     
                       REMAINDER WS-REMAINDER.                                  
           IF  WS-REMAINDER = 0                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 366                            
                  ADD 634                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE                 
              END-IF                                                            
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF  WS-TERMS-COMP-DAY GREATER THAN 365                            
                  ADD 635                 TO WS-TERMS-COMP-DATE
