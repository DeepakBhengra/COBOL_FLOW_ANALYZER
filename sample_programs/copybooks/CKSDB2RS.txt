       8100-CHECKPOINT-ROUTINE.                                                 
      *----------------------------------------------------------------*MXV22308
                                                                        MXV22308
      *    CHECKPOINTS ARE TAKEN WHEN CHECKPOINT COUNT                  MXV22308
      *    IS GREATER THAN THE COUNT LIMIT(MAKE SURE DCLWW-CHKPT-AREA   MXV22308
      *    HAS BEEN FORMATTED ALREADY) OR 10 SECONDS HAVE ELAPSED       MXV22308
      *    SINCE THE LAST CHECKPOINT CALL.                              MXV22308
                                                                        MXV22308
      *> ANYTHING UPDATED ?                                             MXV22308
           IF CHKDB2-CHKP-COUNT > ZERO                                  MXV22308
              PERFORM GET-CURRENT-TIME                                  MXV22308
              COMPUTE CHKDB2-SECONDS-ELAPSED =                          MXV22308
                      CHKDB2-SECONDS - CHKDB2-SECONDS-PREVIOUS          MXV22308
              IF CHKDB2-CHKP-COUNT      > SYTPGM-CHKPT-FREQ-NBR OR      MXV22308
                 CHKDB2-SECONDS-ELAPSED > CHKDB2-LIMIT-TIME             MXV22308
                    PERFORM 8110-UPDATE-SYTARA                          MXV22308
                    IF SQLCODE-NOT-FOUND                                MXV22308
                       PERFORM 8120-INSERT-SYTARA                       MXV22308
                    END-IF                                              MXV22308
                    PERFORM 8130-DB2-COMMIT                             MXV22308
              END-IF                                                    MXV22308
           END-IF.                                                      MXV22308
