      *--------------------------------------------------------------*          
      * GET UNIQUE CUSHED + CUSHEDOF PATH CALL BY PRIMARY INDEX                 
      *--------------------------------------------------------------*          
      *    05/05/23  MXR NEW                                            MXR23125
      *--------------------------------------------------------------*          
           CALL 'CBLTDLI' USING        GU                                       
                                       CUPCB                                    
                                       CU-CUSHED                                
                                       WS-CUSHED-SSA                            
                                       WS-CUSHEDOF-SSA.                         
           IF CUPCB-NORESP                                                      
               MOVE 'Y'                TO  WS-CUSHEDOF-IO-SW                    
               MOVE 'Y'                TO  WS-CUSHED-IO-SW                      
           ELSE                                                                 
               IF CUPCB-NOTFND                                                  
                   MOVE 'N'            TO  WS-CUSHEDOF-IO-SW                    
                   IF CUPCB-SEG-NAME EQUAL 'CUSHED  '                           
                       MOVE 'Y'        TO  WS-CUSHED-IO-SW                      
                   ELSE                                                         
                       MOVE 'N'        TO  WS-CUSHED-IO-SW                      
               ELSE                                                             
                   MOVE 'C203'         TO  WS-ABCODE                            
                   MOVE CUPCB-STS      TO  WS-STATUS                            
                   MOVE 'DL/1'         TO  WS-ERROR-CODE                        
                   MOVE SPACES         TO  WS-ERROR-OPTIONAL-DATA               
                   MOVE CUPCB          TO  WS-ERROR-DL1-PCB                     
                   MOVE WS-ABCODE      TO  WS-ERROR-DL1-ABCODE                  
                   PERFORM 999-ABEND.
