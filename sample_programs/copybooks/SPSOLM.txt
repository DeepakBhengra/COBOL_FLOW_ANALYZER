      *  SPSOLM - PROCEDURE COPYBOOK TO INVOKE SPP400 OLM DISC RTN    *

      * ------------------------------------------------------------- *         
      * REVISIONS:                                                    *

      *                                                                         
      *-----------------------------------------------------------------        
      *  START THE SPP400 TRANSACTION                                           
      *-----------------------------------------------------------------        
       SPSOLM-START-SPP400-TXN.                                                 
      *                                                                         
           MOVE ORH-COMPANY               TO TB-OLM-CO-CODE.                    
           CALL 'SYU510'               USING TB-OLM-PROC-IA.                    
      *                                                                         
           IF TB-OLM-NORESP                                                     
              MOVE ORH-COMPANY            TO SF-CALC-COMPANY-CODE               
              MOVE ORH-BR-NBR             TO SF-CALC-BR-NBR                     
              MOVE ORH-ORDER-NBR          TO SF-CALC-ORD-NBR                    
              MOVE ORSH-DIST-NBR          TO SF-CALC-ORD-DIST-NBR               
              MOVE ORSH-SHIP-NBR          TO SF-CALC-ORD-SHIP-NBR               
              MOVE SPACES                 TO SF-CALC-UPDATES-APPLIED-SW         
              MOVE WS-SP40-FR-EVENT       TO SF-CALC-FROM-EVENT                 
              MOVE WS-SP40-FR-PROGRAM     TO SF-CALC-FROM-PROGRAM               
              MOVE TC-OPER-ID             TO SF-CALC-OPID                       
                                                                                
              EXEC CICS START                                                   
                   TRANSID ('SP40')                                             
                   FROM    (SF-CALC-COMMAREA)                                   
                   LENGTH  (SF-CALC-TRIG-LENGTH)                                
                   RESP    (WS-EIBRESP)                                         
                   PROTECT                                                      
              END-EXEC                                                          
                                                                                
           END-IF.                                                              
      *                                                                         
       SPSOLM-START-SPP400-TXN-EXIT.                                            
                               EXIT.                                            
      *
