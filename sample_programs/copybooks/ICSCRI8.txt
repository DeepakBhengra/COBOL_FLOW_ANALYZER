      *  THIS COPYBOOK CONTAINS THE LOGIC TO DECIDE IF THE INSBRN COST *        
      *  FIELDS SHOULD BE OVERLAID BY THE RI COST FIELDS.  SINCE THIS  *        
      *  COPYBOOK ALSO MAKES REFERENCE TO THE ACOP PARAGRAPH           *        
      *  A123-MOVE-ACOP-COST-INSBRN, ONLY USE THIS COPYBOOK IN A       *        
      *  PROGRAM THAT ALSO HAS THE COPYBOOK ICSCOP2 IN IT.             *        
      *                                                                *        
      * REVISION INFO:                                                 *        
      * 07/30/01  WFP  NEW COPYBOOK.                                   *WFP01211
      *                                                                *        
      *================================================================*        
      *                                                                *        
      ***                                                         ***           
      ***  DETERMINE IF ANY COSTS SHOULD OVERLAY THE INSHED       ***           
      ***  FIELDS.                                                ***           
      ***                                                         ***           
                                                                                
       9900-ICRI-SET-IH-COSTS-INSBRN.                                           
                                                                                
      *===>  IF THE INSHED IS FROM THE LOCAL COMPANY, THEN                      
      *===>  THE COST DATA SHOULD BE OVERLAID, IF PREVIOUSLY                    
      *===>  FOUND AND CONVERTED.                                               
                                                                                
      *===>  SINCE THE ACOP WOULD HAVE BEEN CALCULATED USING                    
      *===>  THE CONVERTED REMOTE (IH) COSTS, THEN CHECK TO                     
      *===>  SEE IF THE ACOP DATA SHOULD OVERLAY THE INSBRN COSTS.              
                                                                                
           IF WS-INAL2-CO = ICRI-INV-OWNING-COMPANY                             
            AND WS-INAL2-CO > SPACES                                            
              IF ICRI-DATA-FOUND                                                
                 PERFORM 9700-ICRI-IH-COSTS-TO-INSBRN                           
                    THRU 9700-ICRI-EXIT                                         
              END-IF                                                            
              PERFORM A123-MOVE-ACOP-COST-INSBRN.                               
                                                                                
       9900-ICRI-EXIT.                                                          
                                                                                
           EXIT.
