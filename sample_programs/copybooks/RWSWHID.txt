      *              I N V E N T O R Y   D O W N L O A D              *

      *                                                               *         
      *  PURPOSE:  CSW PROJECT -                                      *         
      *            WHEN CHANGE TO INVENTORY INFORMATION OCCURS        *         
      *            WRITE A RWLTRN RECORD TO INITIATE THE APPC         *         
      *            PROGRAM THAT WILL SEND INFORMATION TO CSW          *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  NOTE 1:   PROGRAMS USING THIS COPYBOOK MUST HAVE ACCESS TO   *         
      *            THE FOLLOWING:                                     *         
      *            DATABASE: INVETORY                                 *         
      *            SEGMENTS: HEADER                                   *         
      *            COPYBOOKS: INSHED, RWLTRN, RWKTRN, SYXCOM          *         
      *****************************************************************         
      * ============================================================= *         
      *  RWSWHID - PROCESS RWLTRN RECORD                              *         
      *  07/24/00  LKS  CREATION OF COPYBOOK                          * LKS00206
      * ============================================================= *         
                                                                                
           MOVE SPACES                 TO RWTRN-RWRTRN.                         
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO RWTRN-START-DATE                      
                                          RWTRN-START-TIME                      
                                          RWTRN-DELAY-TIME                      
                                          RWTRN-APPC-ATTEMPTS.                  
           MOVE INI-COMPANY            OF INI-INSHED                            
                                       TO RWTRN-COMPANY.                        
      ** BRANCH OF 00 INDICATES THAT DATA IS TO BE SEND TO ALL CSW DC'S         
           MOVE '00'                   TO RWTRN-BR-NBR.                         
           MOVE 'O'                    TO RWTRN-FLOW-CODE.                      
           MOVE '3'                    TO RWTRN-PRIORITY.                       
           MOVE EIBTIME                TO RWTRN-SEQ-NBR                         
                                          RWTRN-START-TIME.                     
           MOVE ZEROS                  TO RWTRN-SUB-SEQ.                        
           MOVE 'WHID'                 TO RWTRN-TRANS-CODE.                     
           MOVE EIBDATE                TO RWTRN-START-DATE.                     
           MOVE 1                      TO RWTRN-APPC-ATTEMPTS.                  
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO RWTRN-DELAY-TIME.                     
           MOVE 'C'                    TO RWTRN-INV-UPDATE-SW.                  
           MOVE INI-INV-PART-NBR       OF INI-INSHED                            
                                       TO RWTRN-PART-NBR.                       
           MOVE '14'                   TO RWTRN-TRANS-TYPE.                     
      *                                   RWTRN-INV-RESET.                      
           MOVE RWTRN-KEY-AREA         TO WS-RWTRN-KEY-AREA.
