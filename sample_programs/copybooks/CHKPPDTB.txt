       9100-CHECKPOINT-ROUTINE.                                                 
      ******************************************************************RMF95129
                                                                                
      *    THIS ROUTINE DETERMINES IF A CHECKPOINT IS READY TO BE       MXV22308
      *    TAKEN. CHECKPOINTS ARE TAKEN WHEN THE CHECKPOINT COUNT       MXV22308
      *    IS GREATER THAN THE COUNT LIMIT OR XX SECONDS HAVE ELAPSED   MXV22308
      *    SINCE THE LAST CHECKPOINT CALL.                              MXV22308
                                                                        MXV22308
           MOVE 'N'                       TO  CHKP-TAKEN-SW.            MXV22308
                                                                        MXV22308
      *> ANYTHING UPDATED ?                                             MXV22308
           IF CHKP-COUNT > ZERO                                         MXV22308
              PERFORM GET-CURRENT-TIME                                  MXV22308
              COMPUTE CHKP-SECONDS-ELAPSED =                            MXV22308
                      CHKP-SECONDS - CHKP-SECONDS-PREVIOUS              MXV22308
              IF CHKP-COUNT           > CHKP-LIMIT      OR              MXV22308
                 CHKP-SECONDS-ELAPSED > CHKP-LIMIT-TIME                 MXV22308
                    MOVE 'N'           TO  CHKP-BACKOUT-TO-BEGINNING-SW MXV22308
                    PERFORM 9200-CHECKPOINT-CALL                        MXV22308
              END-IF                                                    MXV22308
           END-IF.                                                      MXV22308
