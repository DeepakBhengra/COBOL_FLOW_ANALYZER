      * BASERATE INIT  ----------------->  COPY DSIINIT.               *        
      *                                                                *        
      * THIS COPYBOOK CONTAINS THE CODE TO INITIALIZE THE              *        
      * TEMP STORAGE (TSBR) AREA USED IN THE BASERATE LINK.            *        
      ******************************************************************        
      * REVISIONS:                                                              
      * 01-15-98 TFM  CHANGE CA-ALLOCATION FROM '/' TO ' ' TO WORK WITH TFM99032
      *               REL 9.01.                                         TFM98288
      * 10-01-96 TFM  FIX FN LOGIC                                      TFM96275
      * 03-14-96 TFM  SET UP COMMAREA FOR DSILINK.                      TFM96074
      * 08-07-95 GEH  INITIALIZE 'FN' SWITCH                            GEH95219
      * 05-18-95 GEH  INITIALIZE GROUND FREIGT OUT AMOUNT FIELD         GEH95136
      * 05-01-95 GEH  TEMP CHG TO GET BASE RATE TO WORK....             GEH95121
      *               INITIALIZE WS-DSI-ERRMSG TO SPACES.               GEH95121
      ******************************************************************        
                                                                                
      *   NOTE: IF USING THIS IN BATCH COBOL, YOU WILL NEED TO ADD:             
      *   77  TC-OPER-ID                PIC X(04).                              
                                                                                
        D1000-DSI-INIT.                                                         
                                                                                
      * TEMP STORAGE FIELDS NEEDED IN THE LINK COMMAND:                         
           MOVE 'DSBR'               TO TSAR-TS-BASE-ID.                        
           MOVE TC-OPER-ID           TO TSAR-TS-OPERID.                 TFM96074
           MOVE ZERO                 TO TSAR-TS-ITEM.                           
                                                                                
      * INITIALIZE ALL FIELDS IN TSBR THAT ARE CONSTANT FOR IMI:                
           INITIALIZE                   TSBR-DATA-AREA.                         
           MOVE TSAR-TEMP-STORAGE-ID TO TSBR-ID.                                
           MOVE SPACES               TO TSBR-CLIENT-ID.                         
           MOVE SPACES               TO TSBR-SHIPMENT-NUMBER                    
                                        TSBR-FROM-ZIP-CODE                      
                                        TSBR-TO-ZIP-CODE.                       
           MOVE 'S'                  TO TSBR-SYSTEM.                            
           MOVE 'CC'                 TO TSBR-FROM-CUST-CODE-TYPE.               
           MOVE 'CC'                 TO TSBR-TO-CUST-CODE-TYPE.                 
           MOVE 'P'                  TO TSBR-PREPAID-COLLECT-FLAG.              
           MOVE ZEROS                TO TSBR-FROM-ACTUAL-DSI-NUMBER             
                                        TSBR-FROM-PARENT-DSI-NUMBER             
                                        TSBR-FROM-SPLC.                         
           MOVE ZEROS                TO TSBR-TO-ACTUAL-DSI-NUMBER               
                                        TSBR-TO-PARENT-DSI-NUMBER               
                                        TSBR-TO-SPLC.                           
           MOVE ZEROS                TO TSBR-MILEAGE.                           
           MOVE 'L'                  TO TSBR-MOVE-FLAG.                         
           MOVE '1'                  TO TSBR-TYPE-HAUL.                         
           MOVE 'Y'                  TO TSBR-COD-DISCOUNT.                      
           MOVE 'N'                  TO TSBR-COD-BILL.                          
           MOVE 'Y'                  TO TSBR-APPLY-ARBS.                        
           MOVE ZERO                 TO TSBR-RATE-EFF-DATE                      
                                        TSBR-SHIPMENT-DATE-JUL                  
                                        TSBR-UPS-ZONE                           
                                        TSBR-KEYED-DISCOUNT                     
                                        TSBR-KEYED-ADD-ON-CHARGE                
                                        TSBR-KEYED-DECREASE                     
                                        TSBR-KEYED-INCREASE.                    
           MOVE ZEROS                TO TSBR-RATES-TYPE.                        
                                                                                
      * FOR CARRIER SELECT ONLY:                                                
           MOVE '/'                  TO TSBR-CA-CARR-TYPE.                      
           MOVE '2'                  TO TSBR-CA-PREFERENCE.                     
           MOVE '/'                  TO TSBR-CA-TRANSIT.                        
           MOVE '/'                  TO TSBR-CA-SERVICE.                        
           MOVE '1'                  TO TSBR-CA-COST.                           
           MOVE ' '                  TO TSBR-CA-ALLOCATION.             TFM99032
           MOVE 'D'                  TO TSBR-CA-TRANSIT-TYPE.                   
           MOVE 001                  TO TSBR-CA-TRANSIT-LOW.            GEH95121
           MOVE 999                  TO TSBR-CA-TRANSIT-HIGH.                   
      * IF TSBR-CA-TRANSIT IS BLANK, CARRIER-SELECT WILL NOT USE                
      *   TRANSIT AS A SELECTION CRITERIA...AND...IT WILL NOT RETURN            
      *   TRANSIT DAYS.                                                         
                                                                                
      * INITIALIZE PRODUCT TABLE:                                               
           SET TSBR-PRD-INDEX TO +1.                                            
           MOVE 1      TO TSBR-NUMBER-OF-PRODUCTS.                              
           MOVE SPACES TO TSBR-PROD-ENTRY (TSBR-PRD-INDEX).                     
           MOVE ZEROS  TO TSBR-PROD-NUMBER (TSBR-PRD-INDEX)                     
                          TSBR-PROD-NUMBER-SUFFIX (TSBR-PRD-INDEX)              
                          TSBR-PROD-RATED-CLASS-NUMBER (TSBR-PRD-INDEX).        
           MOVE '92.5' TO TSBR-PROD-CODE (TSBR-PRD-INDEX).                      
           MOVE ZEROS  TO TSBR-PROD-WEIGHT (TSBR-PRD-INDEX).                    
      * WEIGHT NEEDS TO BE FILLED IN AS PART OF CALLING PROGRAM LOGIC.          
      * IF MORE THAN 1 PRODUCT IS BEING RATED AT ONE TIME (LIKE                 
      *       FOR UPS PARCEL PRICING), THEN TSBR-PRD-INDEX AND                  
      *       TSBR-NUMBER-OF-PRODUCTS MUST BE INCREASED TO THE TOTAL.           
      *       AND TSBR-PROD-WEIGHT MUST BE FILLED IN FOR EACH PROD.             
                                                                                
      * DATE ONLY NEEDED IN CASE OF CHANGE IN TARIFF EFFEC DATE:                
           CALL 'SYU002' USING        WS-JUL-DATE.                              
           CALL 'SYU001' USING        WS-JUL-DATE                               
                                      WS-DSI-TODAYS-DATE.                       
           MOVE WS-DSI-MM TO TSBR-SHIPMENT-MONTH.                               
           MOVE WS-DSI-DD TO TSBR-SHIPMENT-DAY.                                 
           MOVE WS-DSI-YY TO TSBR-SHIPMENT-YEAR.                                
           MOVE SPACES                 TO WS-DSI-ERRMSG.                TFM96275
      *                                   WS-DSI-FN-CC-SW.              TFM96275
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO WS-DSI-GROUND-FRT-OUT-AMT.    GEH95138
                                                                                
        D1000-EXIT.                                                             
              EXIT.
