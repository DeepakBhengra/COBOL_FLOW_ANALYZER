      *    THIS PROCEDURE DIVISION ROUTINE REQUIRES USAGE OF WORKING-  *NCL97272
      *    STORAGE COPYBOOKS 'VSAMSTAT' & 'ERXPARM'.  FOR BATCH        *        
      *    PROGRAMS, 'ERXBATCH' COPYBOOK SHOULD ALSO BE USED IN THE    *        
      *    PROCEDURE DIVISION.  IT IS ASSUMED THAT THE STATUS FROM THE *        
      *    MOST RECENT VSAM-FILE ACCESS IS PRESENT IN DATA-ITEM        *        
      *    'VSAM-STATUS', EITHER AS RESULT OF AN ELEMENTARY MOVE OR BY *        
      *    USING 'FILE STATUS IS VSAM-STATUS' AS PART OF THE FILE      *        
      *    SELECT STATEMENT.  BEFORE PERFORMING, MOVE FILE-ID AND      *        
      *    FUNCTION-ID TO APPROPRIATE 'WS-ERROR-' DATA ITEMS.          *        
      ******************************************************************        
      *    REVISIONS:                                                  *        
      ******************************************************************        
      * 10/09/97 NCL PUT STATUS-DESCRIPTION IN WS-ERROR-MESSAGE-1.     *NCL97282
      * 09/29/97 NCL BUILD STANDARD VSAM STATUS CHECK/ABEND ROUTINE.   *NCL97272
      ******************************************************************        
      *                                                                *        
       998-SRCH-VSAM-STAT-TBL-ABEND.                                            
           SEARCH  ALL  VSAM-IO-CODES                                           
               AT END                                                           
                   MOVE 'UNKNOWN STATUS CODE' TO WS-ERROR-MESSAGE-1     NCL97282
               WHEN  VSAM-STATUS-CODE (VSAM-INDEX) EQUAL VSAM-STATUS            
                   MOVE VSAM-STATUS-KEY-DESC (VSAM-INDEX)                       
                                              TO WS-ERROR-MESSAGE-1     NCL97282
           END-SEARCH.                                                          
                                                                                
           MOVE 'VSAM'                        TO WS-ERROR-CODE.                 
           MOVE VSAM-STATUS                   TO WS-ERROR-FILE-STAT.            
           MOVE '16'                          TO WS-ERROR-LEVEL.                
                                                                                
           PERFORM 999-ABEND.
