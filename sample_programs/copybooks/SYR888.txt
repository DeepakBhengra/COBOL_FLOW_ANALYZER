      *  PROGRAMS:  SYV888 - FORMATS AMOUNTS FOR FILE STORAGE BASED    *        
      *                      ON CURRENCY CODE                          *        
      *  PURPOSE:   1.) RETURN PROPERLY FORMATTED VALUES FOR 0-        *        
      *                 AND 2-DECIMAL CURRENCIES                       *        
      *             2.) RETURN BASIC CALCULATION RESULTS FOR AMOUNTS   *        
      *                 IN CORRECT 0- AND 2-DECIMAL CURRENCIES         *        
      *             3.) RETURN PROPERLY CONVERTED LOCAL TO FOREIGN     *        
      *                 OR FOREIGN TO LOCAL AMOUNTS FOR 0- AND 2-DEC   *        
      *                 CURRENCIES                                     *        
      *                                                                *

      * USAGE EXPLANATION:                                             *        
      *                                                                *DOK01114
      * CALL FORMAT(S): BATCH - CALL 'SYV888' USING SY888-PARM-DATA   * DOK01114
      *                 CICS  - CALL 'SYU888' USING DFHEIBLK          * DOK01114
      *                                             DFHCOMMAREA       * DOK01114
      *                                             SY888-PARM-DATA   * DOK01114
      * !!IMPORTANT!! - THE ROUTINE ASSUMES 2-DECIMAL FORMAT UNLESS   * DOK01114
      *               IT IS OVERRIDDEN USING THE VARIOUS DECIMAL      * DOK01114
      *               OVERRIDES:                                      * DOK01114
      *               SY888-IN-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE,                  * DOK01114
      *               SY888-OUT-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE, AND             * DOK01114
      *               SY888-OUT-CMP-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE.                 * DOK01114
      *               - REGARDLESS OF WHICH WAY YOU WISH TO CONVERT   * DOK01114
      *               AN AMOUNT (FOREIGN TO LOCAL, OR LOCAL TO        * DOK01114
      *               FOREIGN), LOAD YOUR LOCAL CURRENCY (AS FOUND    * DOK01114
      *               ON THE CORP FILE) TO THE CURRENCY-CODE FIELD    * DOK01114
      *               AND THE FOREIGN CURRENCY TO THE OTHER; IF THE   * DOK01114
      *               VALUE YOU WISH TO CONVERT IS A FOREIGN VALUE,   * DOK01114
      *               SET FLAG SY888-FOREIGN-TO-LOCAL-OVERRIDE TO 'F' * DOK01114
      *                                                               * DOK01114
      * BASIC EXAMPLE 1: TO OBTAIN THE PROPER UNIT PRICE IN LOCAL      *        
      *                  CURRENCY (THE CURRENCY YOU WOULD EXPECT TO    *        
      *                  FIND ON TB-CPO-CURRENCY-CODE FOR THAT COMPANY)*        
      *                  (ASSUMING UNIT-PRICE IS A 4-DEC FIELD)        *DOK01114
      *       INITIALIZE SY888-INPUT-FIELDS                            *        
      *       MOVE CURRENCY CODE TO SY888-CURRENCY-CODE                *        
      *       MOVE 4             TO SY888-IN-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE      *        
      *       MOVE 4             TO SY888-OUT-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE     *        
      *       MOVE UNIT PRICE    TO SY888-IN-AMT-4-DEC                 *        
      *       CALL 'SYV888'  USING SY888-PARM-DATA        (STATIC CALL)*        
      *                 (THE CALL COULD ALSO BE DYNAMIC)               *DOK01114
      *       IF SYV888-OUT-ERR-FLDS  =  SPACES                        *        
      *          MOVE SY888-OUT-AMT-4-DEC  TO PGM-UNIT-PRICE           *        
      *       ELSE                                                     *        
      *          MOVE 'ERR/WARN MESSAGE'   TO OUTPUT-FIELD.            *        
      *                                                                *        
      * BASIC EXAMPLE 2: TO OBTAIN THE PROPER UNIT PRICE IN LOCAL AND  *        
      *                  FOREIGN CURRENCIES                            *        
      *       INITIALIZE SY888-INPUT-FIELDS                            *        
      *       MOVE CURRENCY CODE TO SY888-CURRENCY-CODE                *        
      *       MOVE FOREIGN CURRENCY CODE                               *        
      *                          TO SY888-FOREIGN-CURRENCY-CODE        *        
      *       MOVE CONVERSION RATE (AS FOUND ON TBXCONR)               *        
      *                          TO SY888-CURRENCY-RATE                *        
      *       MOVE 4             TO SY888-IN-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE      *        
      *       MOVE 4             TO SY888-OUT-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE     *        
      *       MOVE UNIT PRICE    TO SY888-IN-AMT-4-DEC                 *        
      *       CALL 'SYV888'  USING SY888-PARM-DATA        (STATIC CALL)*        
      *       IF SYV888-OUT-ERR-FLDS  =  SPACES                        *        
      *          MOVE SY888-OUT-AMT-4-DEC  TO PGM-UNIT-PRICE           *        
      *          MOVE SY888-OUT-FOR-AMT-4-DEC                          *DOK01114
      *                                    TO PGM-FOREIGN-UNIT-PRICE   *DOK01114
      *       ELSE                                                     *        
      *          MOVE 'ERR/WARN MESSAGE'   TO OUTPUT-FIELD.            *        
      *                                                                *        
      *   A.) ONCE THE BASIC FIELDS OF CURRENCY CODE, FOREIGN CURRENCY *DOK01114
      *       CODE AND RATE ARE LOADED, THOSE FIELDS CAN REMAIN INTACT *        
      *       BY SUBSEQUENTLY INITIALIZING ONLY SY888-IN-VAR-FIELDS    *        
      *                                                                *        
      * BASIC EXAMPLE 3: TO OBTAIN THE EXTENDED PRICE IN LOCAL AND     *        
      *                  FOREIGN CURRENCIES FOR AN ITEM                *        
      *       INITIALIZE SY888-INPUT-FIELDS                            *        
      *       MOVE CURRENCY CODE TO SY888-CURRENCY-CODE                *        
      *       MOVE FOREIGN CURRENCY CODE                               *        
      *                          TO SY888-FOREIGN-CURRENCY-CODE        *        
      *       MOVE CONVERSION RATE (AS FOUND ON TBXCONR)               *        
      *                          TO SY888-CURRENCY-RATE                *        
      *       MOVE 4             TO SY888-IN-AMT-NBR-DEC-OVERRIDE      *        
      *       MOVE UNIT PRICE    TO SY888-IN-AMT-4-DEC                 *        
      *       MOVE QTY           TO SY888-IN-QTY                       *        
      *       CALL 'SYV888'  USING SY888-PARM-DATA        (STATIC CALL)*        
      *       IF SYV888-OUT-ERR-FLDS  =  SPACES                        *        
      *          MOVE SY888-OUT-CMP-AMT-2-DEC                          *        
      *                                    TO PGM-EXTENDED-PRICE       *        
      *          MOVE SY888-OUT-CMP-FOR-AMT TO PGM FOREIGN-EXT-PRICE   *        
      *       ELSE                                                     *        
      *          MOVE 'ERR/WARN MESSAGE'   TO OUTPUT-FIELD.            *        
      *                                                                *        
      * BASIC EXAMPLE 4: TO OBTAIN SALES TAX AT THE LINE LEVEL         *        
      *       INITIALIZE SY888-INPUT-FIELDS                            *        
      *       MOVE CURRENCY CODE   TO SY888-CURRENCY-CODE              *        
      *       MOVE EXTENDED PRICE  TO SY888-IN-AMT-2-DEC               *        
      *       MOVE SYLTAX PERCENT  TO SY888-IN-PERCENT                 *        
      *               (ROUTINE WILL DIVIDE THE INPUT RATE BY 100)      *        
      *       CALL 'SYV888'  USING SY888-PARM-DATA        (STATIC CALL)*        
      *       IF SYV888-OUT-ERR-FLDS  =  SPACES                        *        
      *          ADD  SY888-OUT-CMP-AMT-2-DEC                          *        
      *                             TO PGM-ACCUM-TAX                   *        
      *       ELSE                                                     *        
      *          MOVE 'ERR/WARN MESSAGE'   TO OUTPUT-FIELD.            *        
      *                                                                *        
      * BASIC EXAMPLE 5: TO OBTAIN FOREIGN SALES TAX AT THE LINE       *        
      *                  LEVEL                                         *        
      *       INITIALIZE SY888-INPUT-FIELDS                            *        
      *       MOVE CURRENCY CODE   TO SY888-CURRENCY-CODE              *        
      *       MOVE FOREIGN CURRENCY CODE                               *        
      *                            TO SY888-FOREIGN-CURRENCY-CODE      *        
      *       MOVE CONVERSION RATE (AS FOUND ON TBXCONR)               *        
      *                            TO SY888-CURRENCY-RATE              *        
      *       MOVE 'F'             TO SY888-FOR-TO-LOCAL-OVERRIDE-SW   *        
      *               (THIS TELLS THE ROUTINE THE AMT PROVIDED IS A    *        
      *                FOREIGN ONE)                                    *        
      *       MOVE FOREIGN EXTENEDED PRICE                             *        
      *                            TO SY888-IN-AMT-2-DEC               *        
      *       MOVE SYLTAX PERCENT  TO SY888-IN-PERCENT                 *        
      *               (ROUTINE WILL DIVIDE THE INPUT RATE BY 100)      *        
      *       CALL 'SYV888'  USING SY888-PARM-DATA        (STATIC CALL)*        
      *       IF SYV888-OUT-ERR-FLDS  =  SPACES                        *        
      *          ADD  SY888-OUT-CMP-FOR-AMT                            *        
      *                             TO PGM-ACCUM-FOREIGN-TAX           *        
      *       ELSE                                                     *        
      *          MOVE 'ERR/WARN MESSAGE'   TO OUTPUT-FIELD.            *        
      *                                                                *        
      *     A.)  WHEN WORKING A MULTI-CURRENCY ORDER, AMTS MUST BE     *        
      *          ACCUMULATED SEPARATELY IN BOTH LOCAL AND FOREIGN      *        
      *          CURRENCIES (ACCUMULATING IN LOCAL ONLY AND CONVERTING *        
      *          AT A SUMMARY LEVEL CREATES ROUNDING ERRORS)           *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                                                                *        
      *   ROUTINE RESPONSES:                                           *        
      *            SPACES IN SY888-OUT-ERR-FLDS INDICATES THE INPUT    *        
      *                   PROVIDED WAS GOOD FOR THE REQUEST MADE       *        
      *      ERROR RESPONSES:   (SY888-OUT-ERR-CODE)                   *        
      *           (NOT ENOUGH OR TOO MUCH DATA PROVIDED TO ROUTINE TO  *        
      *                   GIVE A VALID RESPONSE)                       *        
      *            '1 ' - REQUIRED INFO NOT PROVIDED                   *        
      *            '2 ' - INVALID CURRENCY RATE PROVIDED               *        
      *            '3 ' - CONVERSION RATE PROVIDED, BUT NO             *        
      *                   VALID FOREIGN CURRENCY GIVEN                 *        
      *            '4 ' - BOTH QTY AND % PROVIDED FOR CALC;            *        
      *                   CAN ONLY REQUEST ONE AT A TIME               *        
      *      WARNING RESPONSES: (SY888-OUT-WARN-CODE)                  *        
      *             (PROCESS COMPLETE, BUT RESPONSE MAY NOT BE         *        
      *                   MEANINGFUL)                                  *        
      *              (NONE AT THIS TIME )                              *DOK01114
