      ****************************************************************          
      *  COPYBOOK:  EDT414                                           *          
      ****************************************************************          
      *                                                              *          
      * OVERVIEW:                                                    *          
      *  USED IN SUB-ROUTINE  EDV414.  EDV414 DETERMINES WHAT THE    *          
      *  CUSTOMER ACCOUNT #, BILL-TO SUFFIX, AND SHIP-TO SUFFIX      *          
      *  SHOULD BE FOR A SPECIFIC PARTNER.                           *          
      *                                                              *          
      *     INPUT: EDV414-INPUT-DATA                                 *          
      *            - EDV414-CO-CD (REQUIRED)                         *          
      *            - EDV414-PARTNER-ID (REQUIRED)                    *          
      *            - EDV414-IN-CUST-NBR (REQUIRED)                   *          
      *            - EDV414-IN-BILL-TO-SFX (OPTIONAL)                *          
      *            - EDV414-IN-SHIP-TO-SFX (OPTIONAL)                *          
      *            - EDV414-STORE-NBR (OPTIONAL)                     *          
      *                                                              *          
      *    OUTPUT: EDV414-OUTPUT-DATA                                *          
      *          - EDV414-OUT-CUST-NBR                               *          
      *          - EDV414-OUT-BILL-TO-SFX                            *          
      *          - EDV414-OUT-SHIP-TO-SFX                            *          
      *          - EDV414-NEW-STOR-FLG                               *          
      *          - EDV414-SALES-HOLD-FLG                             *          
      *          - EDV414-MODULE-RESULT-CD                           *          
      *          - EDV414-MODULE-MESSAGE                             *          
      *          - EDV414-DATABASE-RESULT-CODES:                     *          
      *              - EDV414-SQLCODE                                *          
      *              - EDV414-SQLSTATE                               *          
      *              - EDV414-SQL-MESSAGE                            *          
      *                                                              *          
      *                                                              *          
      *   EDV414 EXAMPLE:                                            *          
      *     INITIALIZE EDV414-COMMUNICATIONS-AREA.                   *          
      *     MOVE PGM-CO-CD TO EDV414-CO-CD.                          *          
      *     MOVE PGM-PARTNER-ID TO EDV414-PARTNER-ID.                *          
      *     MOVE WS-CUST-NBR TO EDV414-IN-CUST-ACCT-NBR.             *          
      *     CALL 'EDV414' USING EDV414-COMMUNICATIONS-AREA.          *          
      *     IF EDV414-STATUS-OK                                      *          
      *     THEN                                                     *          
      *          MOVE EDV414-OUT-CUST-NBR TO EDV407-CUST-NBR         *          
      *          MOVE EDV414-OUT-BILL-TO-SFX TO EDV407-CUST-SFX      *          
      *          MOVE EDV414-OUT-SHIP-TO-SFX TO EDV407-ST-IMI-ST-SUF *          
      *          MOVE EDV414-NEW-STOR-FLG TO WS-NEW-STORE-FLG        *          
      *          MOVE EDV414-SALES-HOLD-FLG TO WS-SALES-HOLD-FLG     *          
      *     ELSE                                                     *          
      *          CONTINUE CHECK FOR DETAILED ERROR CONDITIONS AND    *          
      *          TAKE APPROPRIATE ACTIONS.                           *          
      *                                                              *          
      *            THE PROGRAMMER SHOULD ALWAYS CHECK MODULE RESULT  *          
      *            CODE RETURNED FROM EDV414 TO VALIDTE THAT IT      *          
      *            WORKED PROPERLY (SEE THE COPYBOOK EDT414 FOR A    *          
      *            LIST OF ERROR CODES).                             *          
      *                                                              *
