       SP610-SETUP-EXPL-PLAN-Q-EX-ID.                                           
           INITIALIZE  ORTEXPLN-TS-QUEUE.                                       
           SET ORTEXPLN-IND-EXPLAIN-ID    TO TRUE.                              
           MOVE SYWSPE-R-EXPLAIN-ID (SYWSPSRS-IDX)                              
             TO ORTEXPLN-EXPLAIN-ID.                                            
                                                                                
       SP620-SETUP-EXPL-PLAN-Q-ORDER.                                           
           INITIALIZE  ORTEXPLN-TS-QUEUE.                                       
           SET ORTEXPLN-IND-ORDER-KEY     TO TRUE                               
           MOVE ORH-COMPANY               TO ORTEXPLN-COMPANY                   
           MOVE ORH-ENTRY-DATE            TO ORTEXPLN-ORDER-DATE                
           MOVE ORH-BR-NBR                TO ORTEXPLN-ORDER-BR.                 
           MOVE ORH-ORDER-NBR             TO ORTEXPLN-ORDER-NBR.                
           MOVE ORSH-DIST-NBR             TO ORTEXPLN-ORDER-DIST.               
           MOVE ORSH-SHIP-NBR             TO ORTEXPLN-ORDER-SHIP.               
           MOVE TC-LINE-NBR               TO ORTEXPLN-LINE-NBR.                 
                                                                                
       SP630-SETUP-EXPL-PLAN-Q-DISPL.                                           
           INITIALIZE  ORTEXPLN-TS-QUEUE.                                       
           SET ORTEXPLN-IND-DISPLAY-TSQ   TO TRUE                               
           MOVE ORKSPE-QUEUE-NAME         TO ORTEXPLN-DISPLAY-TSQ-NAME.
