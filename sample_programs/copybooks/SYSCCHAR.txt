      * ***********************************************************             
      *    CONVERT INVALID EBCDIC CHARACTERS ROUTINE.                           
      *                                                                         
      *          ***  NEEDED FOR PROGRAM   ***                                  
      *    COPYBOOK SYWVC01.                                                    
      *    MOVE (TEXT FIELD)          TO VC01-TEXT-AREA.                        
      *    MOVE (TEXT FIELD LENGTH)   TO VC01-LEN.                              
      *                                                                         
      * ****************************************************************        
      * REVISIONS                                                      *        
      * 01/31/06 TKC  CONVERT INVALID CHARACTER                        *TKC06031
      * ****************************************************************        
      *                                                                         
       V010-CONVERT-CHAR.                                                       
                                                                                
           IF VC01-LEN  > VC01-LEN-MAX                                          
               MOVE VC01-LEN-MAX              TO VC01-LEN.                      
           MOVE VC01-OLD-SET                  TO VC01-OLD-TEST                  
           MOVE VC01-NEW-SET                  TO VC01-NEW-TEST                  
           INSPECT VC01-TEXT-AREA(1:VC01-LEN) CONVERTING VC01-OLD-TEST          
                                              TO VC01-NEW-TEST.                 
                                                                                
       V010-EXIT.  EXIT.
