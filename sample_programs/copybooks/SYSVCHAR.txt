      *    COUNT OF VALID CHARACTERS                                            
      *    COUNT OF INVALID CHARACTERS

       V100-CLEANUP-TEXT.                                                       
           IF  VC-SUB-MAX                   > +2048                             
               MOVE +2048                   TO VC-SUB-MAX.                      
           MOVE VC-OLD-SET                  TO VC-OLD-TEST.             MCS97261
           MOVE VC-NEW-SET                  TO VC-NEW-TEST.             MCS97261
           MOVE 'N'                         TO VC-TXT-CHANGED-SW.               
           INSPECT VC-TEXT-AREA(1:VC-SUB-MAX) CONVERTING VC-OLD-TEST    MCS97261
              TO VC-NEW-TEST.                                           MCS97261
                                                                                
       V100-EXIT.  EXIT.
