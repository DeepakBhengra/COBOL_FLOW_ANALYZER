      ******************************************************************        
      *         NAME: EDRUPCPD                                                  
      *=================================================================        
      *04/28/08 JFH INITIAL VERSION                                    *        
      *                                                                *        
      *=================================================================        
      *  THE PURPOSE OF THIS CPYLIB MEMBER IS TO CALCULATE THE CHECK   *        
      *    DIGIT FOR UPC CODE.                                         *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  COPYBOOKS EDRUPCPD AND EDRUPCWS ARE USED TOGETHER TO DO THE   *        
      *    CHECK DIGIT VALIDATION. EDRUPCPD IS THE PROCEDURE DIVISION  *        
      *    MEMBER AND EDRUPCWS IS THE WORKING-STORAGE MEMBER.          *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  THE UPC CODE NEEDS TO BE MOVED TO "UPCWS-UPC-CODE" AND        *        
      *  THE PROGRAM PERFORM PARAGRAPH "UPC-CALC-CHECK-DIGIT"          *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  INITIALIZE THE FOLLOWING FIELDS.                              *        
      *     UPC CODE  ........ UPCWS-UPC-CODE                          *        
      *                                                                *        
      *================================================================*        
      *  EXAMPLE:                                                      *        
      *================================================================*        
      *                                                                *        
      *  WORKING-STORAGE SECTION                                       *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      COPY UPCWS.                                               *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *  PROCEDURE DIVIDION.                                           *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      MOVE MOVE WS-UPC-CODE      TO  UPCWS-UPC-CODE.            *        
      *      PERFORM UPC-CALC-CHECK-DIGIT.                             *        
      *      MOVE UPCWS-UPC-CODE       TO ** YOUR DATA NAME **         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      COPY EDRUPCPD.                                            *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                                                                *        
      ******************************************************************        
       UPC-CALC-CHECK-DIGIT.                                                    
      *                                                                         
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-CODE       TO UPCWS-UPC-WRK.                          
           INSPECT UPCWS-UPC-WRK REPLACING ALL LOW-VALUES BY ' '.               
                                                                                
           IF UPCWS-UPC-WRK = SPACES OR LOW-VALUES                              
              GO TO UPC-CALC-CHECK-DIGT-EXIT.                                   
                                                                                
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (13:1) TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (14:1).                  
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (12:1) TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (13:1).                  
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (11:1) TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (12:1).                  
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (10:1) TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (11:1).                  
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (9:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (10:1).                  
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (8:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (9:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (7:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (8:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (6:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (7:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (5:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (6:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (4:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (5:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (3:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (4:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (2:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (3:1).                   
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK (1:1)  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (2:1).                   
           MOVE '3'                  TO  UPCWS-UPC-WRK (1:1).                   
                                                                                
           MOVE UPCWS-UPC-WRK        TO  UPCWS-UPC-CODE.                        
                                                                                
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(1) = UPCWS-AX(1) * 3.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(2) = UPCWS-AX(2) * 1.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(3) = UPCWS-AX(3) * 3.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(4) = UPCWS-AX(4) * 1.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(5) = UPCWS-AX(5) * 3.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(6) = UPCWS-AX(6) * 1.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(7) = UPCWS-AX(7) * 3.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(8) = UPCWS-AX(8) * 1.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(9) = UPCWS-AX(9) * 3.                               
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(10) = UPCWS-AX(10) * 1.                             
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(11) = UPCWS-AX(11) * 3.                             
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(12) = UPCWS-AX(12) * 1.                             
           COMPUTE UPCWS-WY(13) = UPCWS-AX(13) * 3.                             
                                                                                
           COMPUTE UPCWS-W01      = UPCWS-WY(1) + UPCWS-WY(2)                   
                                  + UPCWS-WY(3) + UPCWS-WY(4)                   
                                  + UPCWS-WY(5) + UPCWS-WY(6)                   
                                  + UPCWS-WY(7) + UPCWS-WY(8)                   
                                  + UPCWS-WY(9) + UPCWS-WY(10)                  
                                  + UPCWS-WY(11) + UPCWS-WY(12)                 
                                  + UPCWS-WY(13).                               
                                                                                
           DIVIDE UPCWS-W01 BY 10                                               
               GIVING UPCWS-W02                                                 
               REMAINDER UPCWS-W03.                                             
                                                                                
           MOVE ZEROS TO UPCWS-CHECK-DIGIT.                                     
           COMPUTE UPCWS-CHECK-DIGIT = 10 - UPCWS-W03.                          
                                                                                
           IF UPCWS-CHECK-DIGIT > 9                                             
               MOVE ZEROES TO UPCWS-CHECK-DIGIT.                                
                                                                                
           MOVE UPCWS-CK-DIGIT-2 TO UPCWS-UPC-CODE-14.                          
                                                                                
       UPC-CALC-CHECK-DIGT-EXIT.                                                
           EXIT.
