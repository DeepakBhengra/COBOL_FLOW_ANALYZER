      *  PROGRAM NAME: ICP837 - DETERMINE ALL ACOPS APPLIED TO A       *        
      *  AND PURPOSE   PARTICULAR ORDER LINE, AND RECALCULATE THE UNIT *        
      *                COST, GIVEN NEW CURRENCY RATE AND AVERAGE COST. *        
      *                                                                *        
      *========>       THIS IS DESIGNED TO OPERATE AS A SUBPROGRAM.    *        
      *========>       THIS IS DESIGNED TO OPERATE AS A SUBPROGRAM.    *        
      *========>       THIS IS DESIGNED TO OPERATE AS A SUBPROGRAM.    *        
      *                AS A RESULT, THIS PROGRAM SHOULD NEVER          *        
      *                INTENTIONALLY ABEND.  FURTHER, EXTRA EFFORT     *        
      *                SHOULD BE MADE TO PREVENT UNINTENTIONAL ABENDS. *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  INPUT:        INTERFACE AREA                                  *        
      *                ICLCOP                                          *        
      *                ICHCOP                                          *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  OUTPUT:       INTERFACE AREA                                  *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  INTERFACE:  THE INTERFACE AREA IS DIVIDED INTO 4 PARTS:       *        
      *              1.)  IACP-PARM-LENGTH - THIS WILL HOLD THE        *        
      *                   LENGTH OF THE INTERFACE IF ICP837 IS         *        
      *                   CALLED DIRECTLY FROM JCL.                    *        
      *                   A CALLING PROGRAM CAN IGNORE THIS AREA.      *        
      *              2.)  IACP-PROCESSING-FIELDS - THIS AREA HOLDS     *        
      *                   THOSE FIELDS THAT ICP837 USES FOR ALL        *        
      *                   EXECUTIONS OF THE PROGRAM.                   *        
      *                   THIS SHOULD NOT BE INITIALIZED BEFORE EACH   *        
      *                   CALL OF ICP837.                              *        
      *              3.)  IACP-INPUT-FIELDS - THIS AREA HOLDS THE      *        
      *                   DATA THAT ICP837 IS TO PROCESS.              *        
      *                   THIS NEEDS TO BE INITIALIZED BEFORE EACH     *        
      *                   CALL OF ICP837.                              *        
      *              4.)  IACP-OUTPUT-FIELDS - THIS AREA HOLDS         *        
      *                   THOSE FIELDS THAT ICP837 RETURNS TO THE      *        
      *                   CALLING PROGRAM.                             *        
      *                   THIS NEEDS TO BE INITIALIZED BEFORE EACH     *        
      *                   CALL OF ICP837.                              *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  CALLING     THERE IS ONLY ONE SEQUENCE OF EVENTS TO USE THIS  *        
      *  ICP837:     PROGRAM:                                          *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                   CALL ICP837 A MINIMUM OF 3 TIMES.  FIRST,    *        
      *                   TO OPEN THE FILES AND TABLES.  SECOND, TO    *        
      *                   CALCULATE THE ACOP AMOUNT.  THIRD, TO CLOSE  *        
      *                   ALL FILES AND TABLES.                        *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                   TO CALL ICP837 IN ORDER TO OPEN FILES:       *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-INPUT-FIELDS.               *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-OUTPUT-FIELDS.              *        
      *                    MOVE 'O '                                   *        
      *                      TO IACP-ACTION.                           *        
      *                    MOVE 'B'                                    *        
      *                      TO IACP-INPUT-IND.                        *        
      *                    CALL 'ICP837'  USING IACP-PROGRAM-INTERFACE *        
      *                    IF IACP-ACOP-MAJOR-OK                       *        
      *                       NEXT SENTENCE                            *        
      *                    ELSE                                        *        
      *                       DISPLAY 'ICP837 - ERROR OPENING FILES'.  *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                   TO CALL ICP837 IN ORDER TO PROCESS AN ACOP:  *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-INPUT-FIELDS.               *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-OUTPUT-FIELDS.              *        
      *                    **                                          *        
      *                    **  MOVE INVENTORY AND ORDER LINE FIELDS    *        
      *                    **  TO ICP837 INPUT FIELDS.  THIS INCLUDES  *        
      *                    **  THE COMPLETE INSHED SEGMENT FOR THE     *        
      *                    **  PART.                                   *        
      *                    **                                          *        
      *                    MOVE 'A'                                    *        
      *                      TO IACP-ACTION.                           *        
      *                    MOVE 'B'                                    *        
      *                      TO IACP-INPUT-IND.                        *        
      *                    CALL 'ICP837'  USING IACP-PROGRAM-INTERFACE *        
      *                    IF IACP-ACOP-MAJOR-OK                       *        
      *                       NEXT SENTENCE                            *        
      *                    ELSE                                        *        
      *                       DISPLAY 'ICP837 - ERROR PROCESSING ACOP' *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                   TO CALL ICP837 IN ORDER TO CLOSE FILES:      *        
      *                    *** NOTE THAT ONLY THE INPUT AND OUTPUT     *        
      *                    *** FIELDS NEED TO BE INITIALIZED           *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-INPUT-FIELDS.               *        
      *                    INITIALIZE IACP-OUTPUT-FIELDS.              *        
      *                    MOVE 'C'                                    *        
      *                      TO IACP-ACTION.                           *        
      *                    MOVE 'B'                                    *        
      *                      TO IACP-INPUT-IND.                        *        
      *                    CALL 'ICP837'  USING IACP-PROGRAM-INTERFACE *        
      *                    IF IACP-ACOP-MAJOR-OK                       *        
      *                       NEXT SENTENCE                            *        
      *                    ELSE                                        *        
      *                       DISPLAY 'ICP837 - ERROR CLOSING FILES'.  *        
      *                                                                *        
      *                                                                *        
      *              THE OVERRIDE TABLE IN THE OUTPUT AREA CAN ALSO    *        
      *              BE INPUT BY THE USER.  IN SUCH A CASE, ICP837     *        
      *              CAN BE SET TO USE THIS DATA.  THIS MEANS THAT     *        
      *              ICP837 WILL NOT GO THROUGH THE PROCESS OF         *        
      *              DETERMINING THE ACOP'S THAT WERE APPLIED TO THE   *        
      *                                                                *        
      *              IN ORDER TO PROCESS WITH THE OVERRIDE TABLE       *        
      *              PROVIDED BY THE USER, FILL IN THE OVERRIDE        *        
      *              TABLE, AND THEN SET IACP-INPUT-IND TO 'P'.        *        
      *              ORDINARILY, IACP-INPUT-IND IS SET TO 'B'.         *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  ICP837      THE INPUT AREA, PARM LENGTH, AND STATIC FIELDS ARE*        
      *  OUTPUT:     LEFT UNCHANGED.                                   *        
      *              THE OUTPUT AREA CONTAINS MANY USEFUL FIELDS,      *        
      *              INCLUDING:                                        *        
      *              IACP-RETURN-CODE - 2 BYTES, FOR MAJOR AND MINOR   *        
      *                                 MESSAGES.                      *        
      *               IACP-RETURN-MAJOR - IF '0', IT IS OK TO USE      *        
      *                                   THE OUTPUT.                  *        
      *                                   IF '1', THEN THE OUTPUT IS   *        
      *                                   SUSPECT, AND SHOULD BE       *        
      *                                   DISCARDED.                   *        
      *               IACP-RETURN-MINOR - IF '0', ALL IS WELL, SO THE  *        
      *                                   OUTPUT IS FINE. IF IT IS NOT *        
      *                                   '0', THE OUTPUT MAY STILL BE *        
      *                                   OK. CONSULT THE MAJOR RETURN *        
      *                                   CODE TO DETERMINE THIS.      *        
      *                                   FOR OTHER VALUES, USE THE    *        
      *                                   CONDITION NAMES TO DETERMINE *        
      *                                   THEIR MEANING.               *        
      *                                                                *        
      *              IACP-RETURN-TEXT - CONTAINS A MESSAGE, SUITABLE   *        
      *                                 FOR DISPLAYING, BY THE         *        
      *                                 CALLING PROGRAM.               *        
      *                                                                *        
      *              IACP-COST-OVERRIDE-INFO                           *        
      *                               - THIS TABLE CONTAINS UP TO 5    *        
      *                                 OCCURRENCES, EACH OF WHICH     *        
      *                                 CORRESPONDS TO A COST          *        
      *                                 OVERRIDE APPLIED TO THE LINE.  *        
      *                               - MOST OF THESE FIELDS APPLY     *        
      *                                 ONLY IF THE OVERRIDE TYPE IS   *        
      *                                 AN 'A' (ACOP).                 *        
      *              IACP-COST-OVERRIDE-AMT                            *        
      *                               - FOR ALL OVERRIDES, THIS FIELD  *        
      *                                 LISTS THE AMOUNT OF THE COST   *        
      *                                 CHANGE ATTRIBUTABLE TO THIS    *        
      *                                 OVERRIDE.                      *        
      *              IACP-UNIT-COST-OUT                                *        
      *                               - THIS IS THE FINAL UNIT COST,   *        
      *                                 AFTER ALL OVERRIDES ARE        *        
      *                                 APPLIED.                       *        
      *              IACP-ADJUSTED-COST-OUT                            *        
      *                               - THIS IS THE NEW ADJUSTED COST  *        
      *                                 FOR THE ORDER LINE.            *        
      *              IACP-ADJ-COST-NO-IR-OUT                           *        
      *                               - THIS IS THE NEW ADJUSTED COST  *        
      *                                 FOR THE ORDER LINE, WITHOUT    *        
      *                                 REGARD FOR INSTANT REBATE.     *        
      *              IACP-ADJUSTED-COST-DATA-OUT                       *        
      *                               - THESE ARE THE FIELDS USED IN   *        
      *                                 CALCULATING THE NEW ADJUSTED   *        
      *                                 COST.  THEY CORRESPOND TO THOSE*        
      *                                 STORED ON INSHED AND ICRCOP.   *        
      *              IACP-TOTAL-OVERRIDE-OUT                           *        
      *                               - THIS IS THE TOTAL OVERRIDE     *        
      *                                 APPLIED TO THE LINE.           *        
      *                                 IT IS THE SUM OF THE           *        
      *                                 IACP-COST-OVERRIDE-AMT         *        
      *                                 FIELDS FROM THE OVERRIDE       *        
      *                                 INFO TABLE.                    *        
      *                                                                *        
      ******************************************************************
