      *                                                               *         
      *  PURPOSE:  CSW PROJECT -                                      *         
      *            WHEN CHANGE TO SALES ORDER BATCHING OCCURS         *         
      *            WRITE A RWLTRN RECORD TO INITIATE THE APPC         *         
      *            PROGRAM THAT WILL SEND INFORMATION TO CSW          *         
      *     NOTE:  IF ORSHED SEGMENT HAS NOT BEEN RETRIEVED DO        *         
      *            COPY REPLACING ORH-COMPANY BY TC-COMPANY OR        *         
      *            OTHER VALID SOURCE FOR COMPANY CODE.               *         
      *****************************************************************         
      * ============================================================= *         
      *  RWSWHSO - PROCESS RWLTRN RECORD                              *         
      *  12/10/03  LLV  CHANGED DEFAULT TO CUSTOMER PRIORITY LOGIC    * LLV03351
      *  10/30/03  LLV  DEFAULT TO CUSTOMER PIRORITY IF SET IN CUSBRN * LLV03303
      *  09/12/03  KAB  IMFIRST CANADA: CHECK RWTRN-OVERRIDE-BR-NBR     KAB03255
      *                 FOR 'SINGLE REGION MULTI BR PROCESSING'.        KAB03255
      *  06/10/99  JRA  CREATION OF COPYBOOK                          * PLB99336
      * ============================================================= *         
           MOVE SPACES                 TO RWTRN-RWRTRN.                         
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO RWTRN-START-DATE                      
                                          RWTRN-START-TIME                      
                                          RWTRN-DELAY-TIME                      
                                          RWTRN-APPC-ATTEMPTS.                  
           MOVE ORH-COMPANY            TO RWTRN-COMPANY.                        
      ** BRANCH OF 00 INDICATES DATA TO BE SENT TO ALL CSW DC'S                 
           IF TB-CSW-SNGLE-REG-MULTI-BR-CONN                            KAB03255
              MOVE TB-CSW-IM-FIRST-BRANCH                               KAB03255
                                       TO RWTRN-BR-NBR                  KAB03255
              MOVE WS-CSW-DC-NBR       TO RWTRN-OVERRIDE-BR-NBR         KAB03255
           ELSE                                                         KAB03255
              MOVE WS-CSW-DC-NBR       TO RWTRN-BR-NBR                  KAB03255
              MOVE SPACES              TO RWTRN-OVERRIDE-BR-NBR.        KAB03255
                                                                        KAB03255
           MOVE 'O'                    TO RWTRN-FLOW-CODE.                      
           IF WS-IMFIRST-OVERRIDE-PRIORITY > SPACES                     LLV03303
               MOVE WS-IMFIRST-OVERRIDE-PRIORITY TO RWTRN-PRIORITY      LLV03303
           ELSE                                                         LLV03303
               MOVE '3'                TO RWTRN-PRIORITY.               LLV03303
           MOVE EIBTIME                TO RWTRN-SEQ-NBR                         
                                          RWTRN-START-TIME.                     
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO RWTRN-SUB-SEQ.                        
           MOVE 'WHSO'                 TO RWTRN-TRANS-CODE.                     
      * FOLLOWING STATEMENT INTENTIONALLY TRUNCATES HIGH ORDER DIGITS           
           MOVE EIBDATE                TO RWTRN-START-DATE.                     
           MOVE 1                      TO RWTRN-APPC-ATTEMPTS.                  
           MOVE ZEROES                 TO RWTRN-DELAY-TIME.                     
           MOVE SPACE                  TO RWTRN-SO-DOWNLOAD-SW.                 
           MOVE WS-CSW-SO-BR-ID        TO RWTRN-SO-BR-ID.                       
           MOVE WS-CSW-SO-NBR          TO RWTRN-SO-NBR.                         
           MOVE WS-CSW-SO-DIST         TO RWTRN-SO-DIST.                        
           MOVE WS-CSW-SO-SHIP         TO RWTRN-SO-SHIP.                        
           MOVE '09'                   TO RWTRN-TRANS-TYPE.                     
           MOVE RWTRN-KEY-AREA         TO WS-RWTRN-KEY-AREA.
