000200*         NAME: SYWBUSD2                                         *        
000300*================================================================*        
000400*  THIS COPYBOOK IS USED WITH SYSBUSD2 TO CALCULATE A FUTURE OR  *        
000500*    PREVIOUS BUSINESS DATE.                                     *        
000600*                                                                *        
000700*  COPYBOOK SYSBUSD2 WILL CALCULATE THE BUSINESS DATE (+/-)      *        
000800*    FROM THE INPUTTED DATE EXCLUDING NON BUSINESS DAYS OF WEEK  *        
000900*    DEFINED IN TBICORP AND DATES CONTAINED IN THE HOLIDAY TABLE.*        
001000*                                                                *        
001100*  WILL CALCULATE WITH BOTH CICS AND BATCH PROGRAMS.             *        
001200*                                                                *        
001300*  THE OUTPUT DATE WILL BE IN SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE.                *        
001400*                                                                *        
001500*  FIELD "SYWBUSD2-ERROR-SW" WILL CONTAIN A CODE INDICATING      *        
001600*    THE RESULTS OF THE CONVERSION. ("Y" IS A NORMAL RESPONSE).  *        
001700*                                                                *        
001800*  YOU WILL NEED TO COPY TBIHDAY, TBICORP AND SYRDATE INTO       *        
001900*    WORKING-STORAGE.                                            *        
002000*                                                                *        
002100*  INITIALIZE THE FOLLOWING FIELDS.                              *        
002200*     COMPANY CODE ..................... SYWBUSD2-COMPANY        *        
002300*     NUMBER OF DAYS (+/-) ............. SYWBUSD2-DAYS           *        
002400*     JULIAN DATE TO BEGIN CALCULATION . SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE      *        
002500*                                                                *        
002600* USAGE:                                                         *        
002700*  BATCH                                                         *        
002800*   COPY SYSBUSD2   REPLACING ==SYUXXX== BY =='SYU500'==.        *        
002900*                   OR                                           *        
003000*  ONLINE                                                        *        
003100*   COPY SYSBUSD2   REPLACING ==SYUXXX== BY =='SYU510'==.        *
