      ******************************************************************        
      ** READ CFSDST SEGMENT VIA CUST NBR, ORDER NBR INDEX S9.                  
      **    REVISIONS:                                                          
      ** 12/14/98 MWO  NEW COPYBOOK.                                    MWO98348
      ******************************************************************        
           CALL 'CBLTDLI' USING        GHN                                      
                                       CFPCB-IDX9                               
                                       CFD-CFSDST                               
                                       WS-CFSDSTS9-SSA.                         
           IF CFPCB-IDX9-NORESP                                                 
               MOVE 'Y'                TO  WS-CFSDST-IO-SW                      
           ELSE                                                                 
               IF CFPCB-IDX9-NOTFND OR CFPCB-IDX9-ENDFILE                       
                   MOVE 'N'            TO  WS-CFSDST-IO-SW                      
               ELSE                                                             
                   MOVE 'I469'         TO  WS-ABCODE                            
                   MOVE CFPCB-IDX9-STS TO  WS-STATUS                            
                   MOVE 'DL/1'         TO  WS-ERROR-CODE                        
                   MOVE SPACES         TO  WS-ERROR-OPTIONAL-DATA               
                   MOVE CFPCB-IDX9     TO  WS-ERROR-DL1-PCB                     
                   MOVE WS-ABCODE      TO  WS-ERROR-DL1-ABCODE                  
                   PERFORM 999-ABEND.
