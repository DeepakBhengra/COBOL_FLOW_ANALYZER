      *************************************************************             
      *** ABEND-ON-ERROR IS USED FOR A PROCESS THAT REQUIRES AN                 
      *** ABEND FOR THE CALLING PROCESS TO HANDLE THE RECOVERY. AN              
      *** EXAMPLE OF THIS IS THE EMS PROCESS. THE EMS QUEUE WILL                
      *** STORE FAILED MESSAGES IF IT RECEIVES AN ABEND FROM SS.                
      ***                                                                       
      *** RETURN-ERROR-ON-ERROR IS USED BY AN APPLICATION WHEN IT WANTS         
      *** TO CALL THE TRANSACTION IN A SYNCHRONOUS PROCESS AND CAN              
      *** HANDLE THE RETRY PROCESS AT THE CLIENT SIDE. THIS PROCESS             
      *** SIMPLY RETURN THE RESULTS WITHOUT ATTEMPTING A RETRY. IT              
      *** WILL ALSO MASK AN ABEND AND RETURN AN ERROR.                          
      ***                                                                       
      *** RETURN-EXISTING-ERROR-ON-ERROR MEANS THAT THE APPLICATION             
      *** WILL NOT MASK THE ABEND/ERROR OF THE CALLED APPLICATION. IF           
      *** THE APPLICATION ABENDS, WWPINB WILL ALSO ABEND. IF THE APP            
      *** RETURNS AND ERROR, WWPINB WILL RETURN AN ERROR.                       
      ***                                                                       
      ******                                                                    
      ***                                                                       
      *** RETRY-IF-ERROR IS USED FOR A PROCESS THAT WILL NOT HANDLE THE         
      *** RECOVERY PROCESS. THIS OPTION IS USED SO THAT THE RETRY IS            
      *** HANDLED ON THE MAINFRAME SIDE. THE CLIENTS ONLY                       
      *** RESPONSIBILITY IS TO GET THE DATA TO THE CICS APPLICATION.            
      ***                                                                       
      *****************************************************************
