000800*=================================================================        
000900*  THE PURPOSE OF THIS CPYLIB MEMBER IS TO CALCULATE THE CHECK   *        
001000*    DIGIT FOR THIRD PARTY FREIGHT BILLING.                      *        
001100*                                                                *        
001200*  COPYBOOKS CUSTHIRD AND CUWTHIRD ARE USED TOGETHER TO DO THE   *        
001300*    CHECK DIGIT VALIDATION. CUSTHIRD IS THE PROCEDURE DIVISION  *        
001400*    MEMBER AND CUWTHIRD IS THE WORKING-STORAGE MEMBER.          *        
001500*                                                                *        
001600*  THE THIRD PARTY ACCOUNT NUMBER NEEDS TO BE MOVED TO           *        
001700*    "CUWTHIRD-ACCOUNT-NBR" AND THE PROGRAM PERFORM PARAGRAPH    *        
001800*    "CUSTHIRD-CALCULATE-CHECK-DIGIT"                            *        
001900*                                                                *        
002000*  WHEN YOU RETURN FROM THE PERFORM CHECK FOR A VALID ACCOUNT    *        
002100*    NUMBER. FIELD "CUWTHIRD-ERROR-REASON" SHOULD BE SPACES.     *        
002200*    IF IT IS NOT; THEN, IT WILL CONTAIN THE REASON FOR REJECTION*        
002300*    OF THE NUMBER.                                              *        
002400*                                                                *        
002500*  INITIALIZE THE FOLLOWING FIELDS.                              *        
002600*     THIRD PARTY ACCOUNT NUMBER........ CUWTHIRD-ACCOUNT-NBR    *        
002700*                                                                *        
002800*================================================================*        
002900*  EXAMPLE:                                                      *        
003000*================================================================*        
003100*                                                                *        
003200*  WORKING-STORAGE SECTION                                       *        
003300*      .                                                         *        
003400*      .                                                         *        
003500*      COPY CUWTHIRD SUPPRESS.                                   *        
003600*      COPY NCARRWS  SUPPRESS.    *** IF NOT ALLREADY BEING USED.*        
003700*      .                                                         *        
003800*      .                                                         *        
003900*  PROCEDURE DIVIDION.                                           *        
004000*      .                                                         *        
004100*      .                                                         *        
004200*      MOVE MOVE WS-ACCOUNT       TO  CUWTHIRD-ACCOUNT-NBR.      *        
004300*      PERFORM CUSTHIRD-CALCULATE-CHECK-DIGIT.                   *        
004400*      IF CUWTHIRD-VALID                                         *        
004500*         MOVE CUWTHIRD-ACCOUNT-NBR TO ** YOUR DATA NAME **      *        
004600*      ELSE                                                      *        
004700*         **  YOUR ERROR PROCESSING  **                          *        
004800*      .                                                         *        
004900*      .                                                         *        
005000*      COPY CUSTHIRD SUPPRESS.                                   *
