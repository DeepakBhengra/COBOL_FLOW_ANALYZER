       A005-CHECK-GSA-PROGRAM-TYPE.                                             
      *                                                                         
           INITIALIZE ICWCOP-GSA-FIELDS.                                        
      *                                                                         
           IF OET-GOVT-GSA                                                      
              MOVE TC-COMPANY                TO ORTCNPT-CO-CD                   
                                                ORTCNVD-CO-CD                   
              MOVE TC-CUST-BR                TO ORTCNPT-BR-NBR                  
                                                ORTCNVD-BR-NBR                  
              MOVE TC-CUST-NBR               TO ORTCNPT-CUST-NBR                
                                                ORTCNVD-CUST-NBR                
              MOVE SPACES                    TO ORTCNPT-CUST-SFX                
                                                ORTCNVD-CUST-SFX                
              IF OET-GOVT-GSN-PARTLINE > SPACES                                 
                 MOVE OET-GOVT-GSN-PARTLINE  TO ORTCNPT-GSA-NBR                 
                                                ORTCNVD-GSA-NBR                 
              ELSE                                                              
                 MOVE OET-GOVT-GSN-HEADER    TO ORTCNPT-GSA-NBR                 
                                                ORTCNVD-GSA-NBR                 
              END-IF                                                            
              MOVE TC-PART-NBR               TO ORTCNPT-PART-NBR                
              MOVE TC-INV-VEND-NBR           TO ORTCNVD-VND-NBR                 
              PERFORM A006-010-READ-ORTCNPT                                     
              IF SQLCODE-NORESP                                                 
      ******     MOVE OET-GOVT-GSA-IND       TO ICWCOP-GOVT-GSA-IND     GAE00034
                 MOVE ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE    TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE-ALT         
                 MOVE ORTCNPT-GOP-PRG-TYP    TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE             
              ELSE                                                              
                 PERFORM A006-020-READ-ORTCNVD                                  
                 IF SQLCODE-NORESP                                              
      ******        MOVE OET-GOVT-GSA-IND    TO ICWCOP-GOVT-GSA-IND     GAE00034
                    MOVE ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE-ALT         
                    MOVE ORTCNVD-GOP-PRG-TYP TO ICWCOP-PROGRAM-TYPE.
