000300* =============================================================           
000400* ------------ DATE ROUTINE BEGIN ---------------------                   
000700     MOVE    SPACE                TO  SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE             
000800                                      SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6.          
000900     IF TC-COUNTRY-CODE = SPACE OR LOW-VALUE                              
001000         MOVE   'MD'              TO  SYU1-COUNTRY-INDICATOR              
001100     ELSE                                                                 
001200         MOVE   TC-COUNTRY-CODE   TO  SYU1-COUNTRY-INDICATOR.             
001300     CALL   'SYU001'           USING  SYU1-INPUT-JULIAN-DATE              
001400                                      SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE             
001500                                      SYU1-EXISTING-GREG-DATE-6           
001600                                      SYU1-NEW-FORMAT-FIELDS.             
001700     MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-MM TO WS-CCYYMMDD-MM                         
001800                                   WS-MMDDCCYY-MM-DIS.                    
001900     MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-DD TO WS-CCYYMMDD-DD                         
002000                                   WS-MMDDCCYY-DD-DIS.                    
002100     MOVE SYU1-MMDDYY-DATE-6-YY TO WS-CCYYMMDD-YY                         
002200                                   WS-MMDDCCYY-YY-DIS.                    
002300     IF WS-CCYYMMDD-YY > 80                                               
002400         MOVE 19 TO WS-CCYYMMDD-CC                                        
002500                    WS-MMDDCCYY-CC-DIS                                    
002600     ELSE                                                                 
002700         MOVE 20 TO WS-CCYYMMDD-CC                                        
002800                    WS-MMDDCCYY-CC-DIS.                                   
002810* ------------------------------------------------------                  
002900*    NOW WS-CCYYMMDD CONTAINS CCYYMMDD IN NUMERIC FORMAT                  
003000*    NOW WS-MMDDCCYY-DISPLAY CONTAINS MM-DD-CCYY FOR DISPLAY
