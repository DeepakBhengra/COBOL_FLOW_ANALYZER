                                                                                
      *****************************************************************         
      *         WORKING STORAGE FOR STRATEGIC PRICING - EXPLAIN PLAN  *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  PURPOSE:  COMMUNICATION AREA CONTAINING THE FULL EXPLAIN     *         
      *            PLAN RETURNED FROM THE PRICING ENGINE WHEN         *         
      *            SYWSPH-RETURN-EXPLAIN-FLAG = 'Y'                   *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  USAGE:    COPY IN YOUR PRICING APPLICATION AFTER THE         *         
      *            STRATEGIC PRICING HEADER AND SKU AREAS.            *         
      *            SET SYWSPH-RETURN-EXPLAIN-FLAG = 'Y' BEFORE        *         
      *            INVOKING THE ENGINE CONNECTOR/PRICING ENGINE.      *         
      *                                                               *         
      *            THIS DATA AREA CAN BE USED TO PASS AN EXPLAIN      *         
      *            PLAN USING A TSQ (SEE UTILITY COPYBOOKS ORSEXPD,   *         
      *            ORSXEXP AND ORSEXPD) OR TO PASS A FULL EXPLAIN     *         
      *            PLAN TO THE ORP697 EXPLAIN WRITER UTILITY.         *         
      *                                                               *         
      *****************************************************************         
      *     REVISION LOG:                                             *         
      * 11/02/04 AXR       *** STRATEGIC PRICING - FULL SOLUTION ***  * AXR04307
      *               1. CREATED COPYBOOK.                            * AXR04307
      *                                                               *         
      *****************************************************************

      ***************************************************************           
      *    THE EXPLAIN PLAN HEADER RESPONSE AREA                                
      ***************************************************************           
                                                                                
           10 SYWSPE-EXPLAIN-DATA-AREA.                                         
                                                                                
              11 SYWSPE-EXPLAIN-HEADER-AREA.                                    
                 12 SYWSPE-EXPLAIN-HEADER-RESPONSE.                             
                                                                                
      *            (MORE THAN ZERO EXPLAIN PLAN ELEMENTS INDICATES              
      *             A SUCCESSFUL EXPLAIN PLAN GENERATION)                       
                    15 SYWSPE-R-NBR-OF-EXPLAIN-ELMTS   PIC 9(02).               
                                                                                
      *            (THE EXPLAIN ID COULD BE SIGNED SOMETIME LATER               
      *             TO DOUBLE THE NUMBER OF UNIQUE VALUES)                      
                    15 SYWSPE-R-EXPLAIN-ID             PIC 9(09).
