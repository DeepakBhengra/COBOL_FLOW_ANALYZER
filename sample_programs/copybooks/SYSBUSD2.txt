      *         NAME: SYSBUSD2                                         *        
      *================================================================*        
      *  THIS COPYBOOK IS USED WITH SYWBUSD2 TO CALCULATE A FUTURE OR  *        
      *    PREVIOUS BUSINESS DATE.                                     *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  COPYBOOK SYSBUSD2 WILL CALCULATE THE BUSINESS DATE (+/-)      *        
      *    FROM THE INPUTTED DATE EXCLUDING NON BUSINESS DAYS OF WEEK  *        
      *    DEFINED IN TBICORP AND DATES CONTAINED IN THE HOLIDAY TABLE.*        
      *                                                                *        
      *  WILL CALCULATE WITH BOTH CICS AND BATCH PROGRAMS.             *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  THE OUTPUT DATE WILL BE IN SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE.                *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  FIELD "SYWBUSD2-RETURN-CODE" WILL CONTAIN A CODE INDICATING   *        
      *    THE RESULTS OF THE CONVERSION. ("Y" IS A NORMAL RESPONSE).  *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  YOU WILL NEED TO COPY TBIHDAY, TBICORP AND SYRDATE INTO       *        
      *  WORKING-STORAGE                                               *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  INITIALIZE THE FOLLOWING FIELDS.                              *        
      *     COMPANY CODE ..................... SYWBUSD2-COMPANY        *        
      *     NUMBER OF DAYS (+/-) ............. SYWBUSD2-DAYS           *        
      *     JULIAN DATE TO BEGIN CALCULATION . SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE      *        
      *                                                                *        
      * USAGE:                                                         *        
      *  BATCH                                                         *        
      *   COPY SYSBUSD2   REPLACING ==SYUXXX== BY =='SYU500'==.        *        
      *                   OR                                           *        
      *  ONLINE                                                        *        
      *   COPY SYSBUSD2   REPLACING ==SYUXXX== BY =='SYU510'==.        *        
      *================================================================*        
      * REVISION:                                                      *        
      *  DATE   PGR   DESCRIPTION                                      *

               IF  SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE IS NUMERIC

                   IF  SYWBUSD2-DAYS NOT NUMERIC                                
                       MOVE '3' TO SYWBUSD2-RETURN-CODE                         
                   END-IF                                                       
               ELSE                                                             
                   IF SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE NOT NUMERIC                            
                       MOVE '1' TO SYWBUSD2-RETURN-CODE                         
                   ELSE                                                         
                       MOVE '2' TO SYWBUSD2-RETURN-CODE.                        
                                                                                
      * CALCULATE BUSINESS DATE                                                 
           IF  SYWBUSD2-NORESP                                                  
               MOVE ZERO TO SYWBUSD2-COUNT                                      
               MOVE SPACES TO WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1                               
               MOVE SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE TO WS-DATE-JULIAN                        
               CALL 'SYU015' USING WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1                          
               IF  NOT WS-DATE-OK                                               
                   MOVE '4' TO SYWBUSD2-RETURN-CODE.                            
                                                                                
           IF  SYWBUSD2-NORESP                                                  
               MOVE 'N' TO SYWBUSD2-ZERO-ADD-SW                                 
               MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSD2-FOUND-SW                                    
               IF  SYWBUSD2-DAYS = ZERO                                         
                   PERFORM 6210-SYSBUSD2-CALCULATE                              
                     UNTIL NOT SYWBUSD2-FOUND                                   
               ELSE                                                             
                   PERFORM 6210-SYSBUSD2-CALCULATE                              
                     UNTIL SYWBUSD2-DAYS = SYWBUSD2-COUNT                       
                       AND (NOT SYWBUSD2-FOUND).                                
                                                                                
       6210-SYSBUSD2-CALCULATE.                                                 
           MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSD2-FOUND-SW.                                       
           IF  SYWBUSD2-DAYS = ZERO                                             
               IF  SYWBUSD2-ZERO-ADD                                            
                   ADD 1 TO SYWBUSD2-COMP-DATE                                  
               ELSE                                                             
                   MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSD2-ZERO-ADD-SW                             
               END-IF                                                           
           ELSE                                                                 
               IF  SYWBUSD2-DAYS < ZERO

           IF  WS-DATE-OK

               IF  SYWBUSD2-BUSINESS-DAY (SYWBUSD2-DOW) = 'Y'           DDR97224
