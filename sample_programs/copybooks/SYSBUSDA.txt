      *      |072996 | TSR  | CREATED THIS COPYBOOK TO CALCULATE A              
      *      |       |      | FUTURE OR PREVIOUS BUSNESS DATE.                  
      *=================================================================        
      *  THIS COPYBOOK IS USED WITH SYWBUSDA TO CALCULATE A FUTURE OR  *        
      *    PREVIOUS BUSINESS DATE.                                     *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  COPYBOOK SYSBUSDA WILL CALCULATE THE BUSINESS DATE (+/-)      *        
      *    FROM THE INPUTTED DATE EXCLUDING SATURDAY, SUNDAY AND       *        
      *    DATES CONTAINED IN THE HOLIDAY TABLE.                       *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  WILL CALCULATE WITH BOTH CICS AND BATCH PROGRAMS.             *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  THE OUTPUT DATE WILL BE IN SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE.                *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  FIELD "SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW" WILL CONTAIN A CODE INDICATING      *        
      *    THE RESULTS OF THE CONVERSION. ("N" IS A NORMAL RESPONCE).  *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  YOU WILL NEED TO COPY TBIHDAY AND SYSRDATE INTO THE WORKING-  *        
      *    STORAGE SECTION IF THEY ARE NOT NOW BEING USED.             *        
      *                                                                *        
      *  INITIALIZE THE FOLLOWING FIELDS.                              *        
      *     CICS SWITCH ...................... SYWBUSDA-SW             *        
      *     COMPAY CODE ...................... SYWBUSDA-COMPANY        *        
      *     NUMBER OF DAYS (+/-) ............. SYWBUSDA-DAYS           *        
      *     JULIAN DATE TO BEGIN CALCULATION . SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE      *        
      *                                                                *        
      *================================================================*        
      *  EXAMPLE:                                                      *        
      *================================================================*        
      *                                                                *        
      *  WORKING-STORAGE SECTION                                       *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      COPY SYRDATE  SUPPRESS.    *** IF NOT ALLREADY BEING USED.*        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      COPY TBIHDAY  SUPPRESS.                                   *        
      *      COPY SYWBUSDA SUPPRESS.                                   *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *  PROCEDURE DIVIDION.                                           *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      MOVE 'Y'                   TO  SYWBUSDA-SW. (CICS ONLY)   *        
      *      MOVE TB-DATE-COMPANY       TO  SYWBUSDA-COMPANY.          *        
      *      MOVE +15                   TO  SYWBUSDA-DAYS.             *        
      *      MOVE TB-DATE-AS-OF-JULIAN  TO  SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE.        *        
      *                                                                *        
      *      PERFORM 6200-SYSBUSDA-DATE THRU 6200-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.       *        
      *      IF SYWBUSDA-NORESP                                        *        
      *         MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE TO  ** YOUR DATA NAME **       *        
      *      ELSE                                                      *        
      *         **  YOUR ERROR PROCESSING  **                          *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      .                                                         *        
      *      COPY SYSBUSDA.                                            *        
      ******************************************************************        
                                                                                
       6200-SYSBUSDA-DATE.                                                      
           MOVE ZERO  TO SYWBUSDA-COUNT.                                SSL99134
           IF NOT END-OF-SYWBUSDA-TABLE                                         
              PERFORM 6220-SYSBUSDA-TABLE THRU 6220-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.              
                                                                                
           IF SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE IS NUMERIC AND                                 
              SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE > ZERO                                         
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE NOT NUMERIC                                 
                 MOVE '1' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW                                  
              ELSE                                                              
                 MOVE '2' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW                                  
              END-IF                                                            
              GO TO 6200-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                         
                                                                                
           IF SYWBUSDA-DAYS IS NUMERIC AND                                      
              SYWBUSDA-DAYS NOT = ZERO                                          
              NEXT SENTENCE                                                     
           ELSE                                                                 
              IF SYWBUSDA-DAYS NOT NUMERIC                                      
                 MOVE '3' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW                                  
              ELSE                                                              
                 MOVE '4' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW                                  
              END-IF                                                            
              GO TO 6200-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                         
                                                                                
      * CALCULATE BUSINESS DATE                                                 
                                                                                
           MOVE SPACES             TO WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1.                      
           MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE TO WS-DATE-JULIAN.                           
           CALL 'SYU015'     USING WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1.                         
                                                                                
           IF NOT WS-DATE-OK                                                    
              MOVE '5' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW                                     
              GO TO 6200-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                         
                                                                                
           PERFORM 6210-SYSBUSDA-CALCULATE THRU 6210-SYSBUSDA-EXIT              
             UNTIL SYWBUSDA-DAYS = SYWBUSDA-COUNT.                              
                                                                                
           MOVE 'N' TO SYWBUSDA-ERROR-SW.                                       
                                                                                
       6200-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                                      
           EXIT.                                                                
                                                                                
       6210-SYSBUSDA-CALCULATE.                                                 
           IF SYWBUSDA-DAYS < ZERO                                              
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 INSERTED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
              CALL 'CAWINUP'                                            RXDY8308
                  USING SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE                              RXDY8308
                        SYWBUSDA-DEFAULT-WINDOW-SPEC                    RXDY8308
                        SYWBUSDA-DISPLAY5-TYPE                          RXDY8308
                        SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE-Y2K                          RXDY8308
                        SYWBUSDA-THIS-YEAR-Y2K                          RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 REPLACED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *       ADD -1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE                              RXDY8308
              ADD -1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE-Y2K                          RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 INSERTED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
              MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE-Y2K TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE         RXDY8308
           ELSE                                                                 
              ADD  1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE.                                     
                                                                                
           MOVE SPACES             TO WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1.                      
           MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE TO WS-DATE-JULIAN.                           
           CALL 'SYU015'     USING WS-DATE-PARAMETER-1.                         
                                                                                
           IF NOT WS-DATE-OK                                                    
              IF SYWBUSDA-DAYS < ZERO                                           
                 MOVE 367 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DAY                                  
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 INSERTED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
                 CALL 'CAWINUP'                                         RXDY8308
                     USING SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR                             RXDY8308
                           SYWBUSDA-DEFAULT-WINDOW-SPEC                 RXDY8308
                           SYWBUSDA-DISPLAY2-TYPE                       RXDY8308
                           SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR-Y2K                         RXDY8308
                           SYWBUSDA-THIS-YEAR-Y2K                       RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 REPLACED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *          ADD   -1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR                           RXDY8308
                 ADD   -1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR-Y2K                       RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
      *      CA-FIX/2000 INSERTED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT(S)         *  RXDY8308
      *Y2K********************************************************Y2K*  RXDY8308
                 MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR-Y2K TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR          RXDY8308
              ELSE                                                              
                 MOVE   0 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-DAY                                  
                 ADD    1 TO SYWBUSDA-COMP-YR                                   
              END-IF                                                            
              GO TO 6210-SYSBUSDA-CALCULATE                                     
           END-IF.                                                              
                                                                                
      *    'NEXT DAY' IS SATURDAY OR SUNDAY, DON'T COUNT IT                     
           IF WS-DATE-DAY-NAME = 'SATURDAY ' OR 'SUNDAY   '                     
              GO TO 6210-SYSBUSDA-CALCULATE.                                    
                                                                                
      *    'NEXT DAY' IS A HOLIDAY, DON'T COUNT IT                              
           MOVE 'N' TO SYWBUSDA-FOUND-SW.                                       
           PERFORM VARYING SYWBUSDA-INDEX FROM 1 BY 1                           
             UNTIL SYWBUSDA-DATE (SYWBUSDA-INDEX) = ZEROES OR                   
                   SYWBUSDA-FOUND                                               
                   IF SYWBUSDA-DATE (SYWBUSDA-INDEX)                            
                                                   = SYWBUSDA-COMP-DATE         
                      MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSDA-FOUND-SW                             
                   END-IF                                                       
           END-PERFORM.                                                         
                                                                                
           IF SYWBUSDA-FOUND                                                    
              GO TO 6210-SYSBUSDA-CALCULATE.                                    
                                                                                
                                                                                
           IF SYWBUSDA-DAYS < ZERO                                              
              ADD -1 TO SYWBUSDA-COUNT                                          
           ELSE                                                                 
              ADD  1 TO SYWBUSDA-COUNT.                                         
                                                                                
       6210-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                                      
           EXIT.                                                                
                                                                                
       6220-SYSBUSDA-TABLE.                                                     
           PERFORM VARYING SYWBUSDA-INDEX FROM 1 BY 1                           
              UNTIL SYWBUSDA-INDEX > 100                                        
                    MOVE ZEROES TO SYWBUSDA-DATE (SYWBUSDA-INDEX)               
           END-PERFORM.                                                         
                                                                                
           MOVE LOW-VALUES              TO TB-HDAY-ENTRY                MCV99271
           MOVE SYWBUSDA-COMPANY        TO TB-HDAY-COMPANY-CODE.                
           MOVE 'N'                     TO TB-HDAY-REQUEST-CODE.                
           MOVE ' '                     TO SYWBUSDA-TBL-SW.                     
                                                                                
           PERFORM VARYING SYWBUSDA-INDEX FROM 1 BY 1                           
             UNTIL END-OF-SYWBUSDA-TABLE OR                                     
                   SYWBUSDA-INDEX > 100                                         
                   IF SYWBUSDA-CICS                                             
                      CALL SYWBUSDA-SYU510 USING TB-HOLIDAY-TABLE-IA            
                   ELSE                                                         
                      CALL SYWBUSDA-SYU500 USING TB-HOLIDAY-TABLE-IA            
                   END-IF                                                       
                   IF TB-HDAY-NORESP                                            
                      IF TB-HDAY-COMPANY-CODE = SYWBUSDA-COMPANY                
                         MOVE  TB-HDAY-DATE TO                                  
                                        SYWBUSDA-DATE (SYWBUSDA-INDEX)          
                      ELSE                                                      
                         MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSDA-TBL-SW                            
                      END-IF                                                    
                   ELSE                                                         
                      MOVE 'Y' TO SYWBUSDA-TBL-SW                               
                   END-IF                                                       
           END-PERFORM.                                                         
                                                                                
                                                                                
       6220-SYSBUSDA-EXIT.                                                      
           EXIT.
