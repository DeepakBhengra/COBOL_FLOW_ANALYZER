      *****************************************************************         
      *  EUWPHN15 - WORKING STORAGE COPYBOOK TO EDIT A PHONE          *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  DESCRIPTION: EDITS AND FORMATS AN INPUT PHONE NUMBER,        *         
      *               IF NO EDIT ERROR, A FORMATTED OUTPUT PHONE      *         
      *               NUMBER IS RETURNED.  IF AN ERROR IS FOUND,      *         
      *               AN 88 LEVEL OF THE ERROR AND AND ERROR MESSAGE  *         
      *               ARE RETURNED.                                   *         
      *               1) IF THE INPUT PHONE NUMBER IS SPACES,         *         
      *                  WS-PHONE-ERR-BLANK IS SET, A MESSAGE IS      *         
      *                  PUT INTO WS-WORK-PHONE-MSG                   *         
      *                                                               *         
      *  EXAMPLE:                                                     *         
      *     WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.                                  *         
      *         COPY EUWPHN15                                         *         
      *                                                               *         
      *     PROCEDURE DIVISION.                                       *         
      *                                                               *         
      *         MOVE PHONEIN  TO WS-WORK-PHONE-COUNTRY                *         
      *         PERFORM 765-EDIT-AND-FORMAT-PHONE THRU 765-EXIT       *         
      *         IF WS-PHONE-OK                                        *         
      *            MOVE WS-WORK-PHONE-OUT TO YOUR-NEW-NUMBER          *         
      *         ELSE                                                  *         
      *            IF WS-PHONE-ERR-BLANK                              *         
      *               MOVE WS-WORK-PHONE-MSG TO YOUR-MESSAGE          *         
      *               PERFORM UP-TO-YOU.                              *         
      *                                                               *         
      *         COPY EUSPHN15                                         *         
      *                                                               *         
      *****************************************************************         
      * REVISIONS:                                                    *         
      * 01/20/2003 ERR INITIAL.                                       * ERR03020
      *****************************************************************

       01  WS-PHONE-NBR-WORKAREA.

           05  WS-WORK-PHONE-IN.                                                
               10  WS-WORK-PHONE-IN-CHAR  PIC  X(01) OCCURS 15                  
                                                     INDEXED WS-P1-IDX.         
           05  WS-WORK-PHONE-OUT.                                               
               10  WS-WORK-PHONE-OUT-CHAR PIC  X(01) OCCURS 15                  
                                                     INDEXED WS-P2-IDX.         
           05  FILLER                     PIC  X(01) VALUE SPACE.               
               88 WS-PHONE-OK                        VALUE SPACE.               
               88 WS-PHONE-ERR-BLANK                 VALUE 'B'.
